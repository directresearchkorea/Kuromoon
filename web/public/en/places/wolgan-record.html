LP listening cafe with a stunning Seongsan Ilchulbong and ocean view.

Wolgan Record is a unique art/theme cafe in Jeju where visitors can enjoy LP music while overlooking the stunning Seongsan Ilchulbong and the beautiful ocean. Guests can select and listen to various genres of LP music, experiencing Jeju's magnificent scenery with both their eyes and ears simultaneously. The cafe offers a selection of coffee, non-coffee beverages, and even alcoholic drinks to suit individual preferences, with the relaxed time spent with LP music being a signature experience. It is highly recommended for friends, couples, or solo visitors seeking to immerse themselves in music and scenery within a soulful atmosphere. Operating from 10 AM to 7 PM, it's an ideal spot to leisurely enjoy the romance of Jeju.
