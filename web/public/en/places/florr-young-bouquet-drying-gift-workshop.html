A workshop in Magok where you can create emotional and special gifts through one-day classes.

Florr Young Bouquet Drying Gift Workshop is a one-day class specialized workshop where you can create emotional and special gifts yourself. Here, you can experience various handmade classes, from drying bouquets to preserve their beauty to delicate wax dioramas. Especially popular among visitors are the bouquet drying class, which allows you to cherish precious memories, and the unique wax diorama class. It's a great place to create special memories with friends or loved ones in a warm and cozy atmosphere, or to foster creativity with children. It is highly recommended for those looking for unique gifts for special occasions or an unusual hands-on experience.
