The Apothecary Diaries special exhibition in Hongdae.

This is a special exhibition for the anime 'The Apothecary Diaries' held on the 2nd floor of Stage Bimil in Hongdae. It recreates spaces from the anime, offering a unique experience for fans, with no re-entry allowed.
