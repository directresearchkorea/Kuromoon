Newly opened casual dining restaurant on Yeonmujang-gil.

Jade & Water Seongsu LCDC branch is a newly opened casual dining restaurant on Yeonmujang-gil, located on the 4th floor of the multi-cultural complex LCDC Seoul. Visitors can enjoy a pleasant indoor date course by exploring various prop shops within LCDC after their meal. While no signature menu is mentioned, the stylish space of LCDC itself serves as a great photo zone. Its trendy and sophisticated interior atmosphere is perfect for couples or friends seeking a special date in Seongsu-dong. Guests using Jade & Water can enjoy 1 hour and 30 minutes of free parking, making visits convenient.
