Miffy character-themed cafe and pop-up store in Yeongdo, Busan.

Miffy Cafe in Yeongdo, Busan, offers a special space themed around the adorable Miffy character. Visitors can browse and purchase a variety of Miffy merchandise, enjoying a delightful visual experience with Miffy. Signature items like limited edition Sailor Miffy goods are a must-see for Miffy fans. With its exciting atmosphere, it's perfect for creating cherished memories with friends, couples, or family, and capturing memorable photos at various photo zones. The cafe provides ample free parking with attendant assistance, ensuring a convenient visit.
