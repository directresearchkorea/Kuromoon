Leather and resin craft workshop in Jeonju Hanok Village.

Amado Leather Workshop, nestled in Jeonju Hanok Village, offers a unique experience space where visitors can create special memories through leather and resin crafts. Guests can enjoy a creative time making leather keyrings and card wallets, or children can participate in engaging resin craft activities. Adults can craft practical leather keyrings and card wallets, while children can complete their own unique works through colorful resin art. With a warm and cozy atmosphere, this workshop is recommended for friends, couples, and families, especially those looking for a special hands-on experience with children. For a smooth experience on weekends, it is advisable to make a reservation via Naver in advance, and please note that the workshop is closed on Sundays.
