50-year-old traditional gimbap restaurant in Gwangjang Market.

Monyeo Gimbap, a venerable restaurant established in 1975, is nestled in the Jeon Alley of Gwangjang Market, boasting 50 years of tradition. This establishment offers a diverse menu, including its original Monyeo Gimbap, savory mung bean pancakes (Nokdu Bindaetteok), crispy shrimp and potato pancakes, and refreshing aged kimchi rolls. Particularly, the spicy perilla oil buckwheat noodles are an excellent pairing with the potato pancakes, providing visitors with a unique culinary experience. It is highly recommended for those looking to savor a variety of Korean street food and pancake dishes with a rich history in Gwangjang Market.
