Muju Ori

🔥 Over 387 Naver blog reviews

A healthy traditional Korean food restaurant near Muju Deogyusan and Gucheondong Valley.

Muju Ori is a renowned local restaurant near Deogyusan Mountain and Gucheondong Valley, specializing in nourishing traditional Korean dishes. Visitors can enjoy a hearty meal here before or after exploring Gucheondong Valley or Deogyusan, making it a perfect stop to rejuvenate after outdoor activities. The must-try signature dish is the herbal native chicken baeksuk, known for its rich broth and tender chicken. With a comfortable and robust atmosphere, it is highly recommended for family gatherings or group meals, especially when dining with parents.
