Experience a fun oven class at Goobne Playtown in Hongdae.

Goobne Playtown in Hongdae offers a unique concept dining experience where visitors can directly participate in Goobne chicken's oven cooking. Guests can enjoy a detailed culinary experience, making and plating their own NEW Original chicken through an engaging oven class. The self-made Original chicken is the signature item, designed for a parent and child pair to complete one whole chicken together, creating unforgettable memories and flavors. This vibrant space is highly recommended for families, providing a perfect setting for creating special memories with children. Reservations are noted to be extremely difficult to secure, so early booking is highly advised for those wishing to visit.
