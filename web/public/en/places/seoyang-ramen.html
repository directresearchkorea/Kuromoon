A unique ramen restaurant near Seoul Station, known for its clean and deep-flavored ramen.

Seoyang Ramen is a unique ramen specialty restaurant located in Jung-gu, Seoul, offering deeply flavored ramen within a small yet clean and simple interior. Visitors can enjoy a comfortable meal in a warm and cozy atmosphere within the well-organized space. The 'Yopu Ramen' is a signature dish here, highly recommended for its clean yet profound broth flavor. Its simple and warm interior makes it suitable for casual dining with friends or couples, as well as for those seeking a quiet meal alone. Conveniently located near Seoul Station Exit 4, it opens at 10:30 AM on weekdays and 11:00 AM on weekends, so checking the operating hours before visiting is advisable.
