Traders Wolgye branch offering wine, whisky, and a T-cafe experience.

Emart Traders Wholesale Club Wolgye Branch is a multi-purpose shopping destination where visitors can enjoy a wide selection of alcoholic beverages and a convenient T-cafe. Guests can explore an extensive list of wines and whiskies for their shopping needs or relax by ordering drinks easily via QR code at the T-cafe. Must-try items include various wines and whiskies like Hakuju, Gekkeikan Junmai, and Kubota Junmai Daiginjo, while the T-cafe's QR order system offers remarkable convenience. With the lively atmosphere typical of a large hypermarket, it's an ideal spot for families or friends to combine grocery shopping with a relaxing break. For the T-cafe, scanning the QR code to order and pay in advance allows for quick pickup without waiting in line, saving valuable time.
