A hidden, atmospheric cafe near Bongcheon Station with a beautiful view.

Labyrinth to Heart is a unique art/theme cafe near Bongcheon Station, designed with a secret base concept. Visitors can enjoy a special visual experience by admiring the beautiful plane tree view outside the window, which is charming even on rainy or cloudy days. The stunning window view itself serves as a signature photo zone, offering a picturesque backdrop. It's an ideal spot for those seeking a quiet, reflective time in a cozy, mysterious atmosphere or for couples looking to enjoy a special ambiance.
