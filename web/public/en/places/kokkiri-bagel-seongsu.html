A famous bagel bakery cafe in Seongsu, offering various bagels and beer.

Kokkiri Bagel Seongsu, renowned as one of Korea's top three bagel spots, is a bakery cafe offering fresh and unique bagels. Visitors can enjoy a variety of bagels paired with refreshing beer, spending a leisurely time. The Ratatouille Bagel and Butter Salt Bagel are highly recommended must-try items, beloved for their distinctive flavors. It's an ideal spot for friends or couples who love bagels to enjoy delicious bread and drinks, and also suitable for a relaxed solo brunch.
