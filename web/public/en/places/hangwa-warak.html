Traditional Hanok dessert cafe near Gangnam/Sinnonhyeon Station.

'Hangwa Warak' is a traditional dessert cafe nestled between Gangnam and Sinnonhyeon Stations, offering a serene Hanok experience amidst the bustling city. Visitors can unwind in a space imbued with beautiful Korean traditional aesthetics, enjoying unique desserts and beverages. The chewy and sweet Gaeseong Juak is a highly recommended signature menu item that guests must try. Its warm and cozy Hanok interior creates a perfect ambiance for gatherings with friends, romantic dates, or even a peaceful solo retreat. Located just a 3-minute walk from Sinnonhyeon Station Exit 6 and an 8-minute walk from Gangnam Station Exit 11, paid parking is also available.
