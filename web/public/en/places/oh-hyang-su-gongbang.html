🔥 Over 60 Naver blog reviews

A perfume workshop in Cheongju offering one-day aroma perfume making classes.

Oh Hyang Su Gongbang in Cheongju is an aroma therapy workshop offering a special perfume making experience to discover one's unique scent. Here, visitors can directly combine various fragrances to create a one-of-a-kind signature perfume, enjoying a healing time indoors even on hot days. Guests can have a special sensory experience through the perfume creation process, and the finished perfume becomes a cherished memory. Its cozy and emotional interior is highly recommended for couples as a date course, and it's also great for creating special memories with friends or enjoying some solo time.
