Colorful donuts and coffee at a popular cafe in IFC Mall.

Knotted IFC Mall Branch is a popular dessert cafe offering sweet delights with its colorful donuts and beverages. Visitors can visually enjoy the vibrant array of donuts and recharge with various flavored donuts paired with decaffeinated coffee or matcha latte. The milk cream donut is a must-try signature item, and the decaffeinated coffee and matcha latte are also highly popular. Its bright and cheerful interior provides a perfect atmosphere for friends or couples to enjoy a sweet time together or to take a break after shopping. Conveniently located within IFC Mall, it offers easy access and parking is available for a hassle-free visit.
