Ader Error's Seongsu flagship store, offering a unique fashion experience.

Ader Error Seongsu Space is the flagship location for the fashion brand Ader Error, renowned for its original designs and experimental sensibility. Visitors can explore Ader Error's latest collections and exciting collaborative products with various brands, including Birkenstock, and the opportunity to see and try on collaboration footwear in person offers a unique delight for fashion enthusiasts. Beyond Ader Error's signature apparel and accessories, limited-edition collaboration items like the Birkenstock shoes are must-see items during a visit, and the space's unique interior itself serves as an excellent photo zone. The sensory and experimental interior perfectly showcases Ader Error's distinct aesthetic, making it ideal for visits with fashion-forward friends or partners, and it is highly recommended for those seeking a unique brand experience combined with shopping. Waiting lines may occur during popular times, so it is advisable to check the crowd status before visiting or choose a less busy period.
