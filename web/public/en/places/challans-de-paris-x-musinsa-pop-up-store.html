Challans de Paris X Musinsa beauty pop-up store in Seongsu-dong.

This collaborative beauty pop-up store by Challans de Paris and Musinsa at Musinsa Beauty Space offers a unique opportunity to experience the latest beauty trends. Visitors can directly try products from various partner brands such as Signic, Cllimé Beauty, Musinsa Standard Beauty, and Fwee, and participate in related events held across Seongsu. Notably, Challans de Paris's Shally Cushion Pact and ampoules are highly recommended by visitors as must-try signature items. It's a perfect destination for friends interested in beauty and fashion to explore new products and have a delightful time, further enhanced by its connection to the Musinsa Megastore opening for a richer shopping experience.
