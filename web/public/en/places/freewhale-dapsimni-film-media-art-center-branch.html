A healing cafe where you can enjoy media art exhibitions and drinks.

Freewhale, located on the first floor of the Dapsimni Film Media Art Center, is a unique healing space combining media art and a cafe, operated by a company supporting the social independence of young adults with borderline intelligence, adding social value. Visitors can enjoy media art exhibitions and beverages in a cool and comfortable atmosphere, offering a refreshing break from daily life. This space provides artistic inspiration alongside a variety of drinks, and is particularly popular among parents from nearby elementary schools, creating a lively ambiance. Its cool and comfortable interior is ideal for conversations with friends or partners, or for enjoying art during solo time.
