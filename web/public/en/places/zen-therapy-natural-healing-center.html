A healing center offering singing bowl sound therapy, meditation, and yoga classes.

Zen Therapy Natural Healing Center is a wellness space dedicated to bringing peace to mind and body through singing bowl sound therapy, meditation, and yoga classes. Visitors can immerse themselves in the deep vibrations and sounds of singing bowls, finding relaxation and healing from daily stress. The singing bowl music therapy is a signature program, offering a unique opportunity to experience harmony between body and mind. With its calm and serene atmosphere, it is recommended for anyone seeking stress relief and inner peace, especially those looking for a complete rest during their solo time. The center offers both regular and one-day classes, making it advisable to inquire and register in advance for desired programs.
