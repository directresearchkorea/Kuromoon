A tea house with a great atmosphere, offering rich matcha and various tea options.

“Chadam by Gallery The Square” is a unique concept tea house located within the Myungwon Museum at Kookmin University, offering a space to experience tranquility and culture through tea. Visitors can savor rich matcha and a variety of tea variations, enjoying a traditional tea ceremony, complemented by traditional desserts like chewy 'dutop-tteok'. The rich matcha and diverse tea variations are signature items here, and the sweet dutop-tteok, which perfectly pairs with bitter tea, is also a must-try delicacy. With its pleasant atmosphere, this spot is an ideal place for quiet conversations with friends or couples, or for individuals seeking a moment of peaceful contemplation. Although access might be somewhat challenging, it is definitely worth a visit for those who wish to deeply enjoy matcha and tea culture.
