Icheon Ceramic Art Village

🔥 Over 20432 Naver blog reviews
✅ Recent status: 운영 중

Art village with over 200 workshops for ceramic experiences and alleyway tours.

Icheon Ceramic Art Village is a unique cultural and artistic village where visitors can experience and appreciate the beauty of Korean ceramic art through over 200 workshops. Enjoy pleasant alleyway tours, admiring diverse ceramic works displayed both inside and outside the beautiful workshop buildings, and discover picturesque scenery, even noted as a filming location for a famous coffee commercial. Here, you can create your own unique piece through various ceramic art experiences, offering a truly special memory. The artistic atmosphere makes it ideal for creating special memories with family, friends, or partners, or for enjoying a peaceful solo retreat. It is recommended to check detailed information through the Icheon Ceramic Art Village Tourist Information Center before visiting.
