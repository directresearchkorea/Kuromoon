Gumirae vintage flea market pop-up in Yeonnam-dong

Gumirae Vintage Flea Market Pop-up is held at Dongjin Parking Lot in Yeonnam-dong. Visitors can find various vintage items, including Patagonia's 90s Synchilla fleece, embodying Patagonia's romantic story of developing fleece from recycled plastic bottles. This summer flea market festival runs for three consecutive weeks, following the Yeonhui Market and Yeonhui Hands Market.
