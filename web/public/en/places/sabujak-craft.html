A workshop in Yeonnam-dong offering unique one-day classes like phone case and chenille doll making.

Sabujak Craft is a unique one-day class workshop located in Yeonnam-dong, offering the joy of creating things by hand. Visitors can spend creative time making their own special works through various experiences such as phone case decoration, chenille doll making, and weaving crafts. The adorable chenille doll making is a signature activity perfect for friends or couples, and the finished piece becomes a cherished memory. Held in a cozy and comfortable atmosphere, these classes are highly recommended for special outings with friends or unique date courses for couples. Reservations can be conveniently made through the Somsi-dang app, and as parking is unavailable, public transportation is recommended.
