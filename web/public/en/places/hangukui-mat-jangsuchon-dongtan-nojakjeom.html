Herbal samgyetang restaurant near Dongtan Nojak Park

Hangukui Mat Jangsuchon Dongtan Nojakjeom specializes in healthy Korean course meals, focusing on herbal samgyetang and baeksuk. Visitors can enjoy a unique culinary experience, revitalizing their bodies with warm, carefully prepared herbal baeksuk and samgyetang made from fresh ingredients. The deeply flavored herbal samgyetang and tender baeksuk are must-try signature dishes, offering both health benefits and delicious taste. With a clean and comfortable atmosphere, it's perfect for family dinners, gatherings with friends, or even a solo visit when you need a healthy boost. It can get busy during lunchtime, so checking the operating hours for a more relaxed visit is recommended.
