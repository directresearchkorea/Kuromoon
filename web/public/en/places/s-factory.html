Information on pop-ups and events at S-Factory, a cultural complex in Seongsu-dong.

S-Factory, a multi-cultural complex located in Seongsu-dong, is a vibrant venue that actively hosts various pop-up stores, exhibitions, and performances. With its spacious layout and modern facilities, numerous brands and artists present creative events, offering visitors a diverse range of experiences. It is highly recommended for those looking to experience the latest cultural trends, as it frequently hosts popular events such as Baek Yerin concerts and ENA's 'X The League' pop-up store. Rather than specific menus or items, the greatest appeal of this venue lies in its exclusive, periodically changing pop-up content and unique performance experiences.
