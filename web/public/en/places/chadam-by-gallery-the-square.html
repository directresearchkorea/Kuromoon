Chadam by Gallery The Square

🔥 Over 329 Naver blog reviews
✅ Recent status: 운영 중

A hanok cafe and gallery offering rich matcha and dutteok in a serene setting.

'Chadam by Gallery The Square' is a unique concept space where a traditional Korean hanok pavilion harmoniously blends with a modern gallery. Visitors can enjoy a special sensory experience, spending tranquil time in a serene atmosphere while savoring rich matcha and diverse tea variations. The chewy and sweet dutteok (rice cake) pairs wonderfully with bitter tea, and the deep matcha is a must-try signature menu item here. The beautiful hanok interior combined with art pieces creates an ideal setting for quiet contemplation or a peaceful date, offering a serene escape. While access might be somewhat challenging, parking information is provided, making it convenient for those traveling by car.
