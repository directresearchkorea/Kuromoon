Omniscient Reader's Viewpoint X Animate Cafe collaboration.

This special collaboration cafe brings together the popular web novel 'Omniscient Reader's Viewpoint' and Animate Cafe. Visitors can discover a variety of collaboration beverages and limited edition merchandise inspired by the characters from the series. Signature drinks like Kim Dokja's Black Sesame Einspanner offer an unmissable experience for fans. This space allows guests to immerse themselves in the work's universe, making it an ideal spot to create special memories with friends or partners.
