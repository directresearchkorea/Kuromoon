Nongshim's 40th anniversary pop-up in Seongsu, offering custom Shin Ramyun.

Celebrating Nongshim's 40th anniversary, the Shin Ramyun Bunsik The Factory pop-up offers a unique experience where visitors can customize and cook their own Shin Ramyun. On the second floor, guests can prepare their ramen as they wish, and a photo zone featuring their favorite ramen provides additional visual enjoyment. The custom ramen, made with ingredients chosen by the visitors, is the signature menu, and the ramen-themed photo zone is a must-visit spot. With its lively and fun atmosphere, this pop-up is highly recommended for friends, couples, and families looking to create special memories with their children. Be prepared for potential queues when visiting the second floor, so it's advisable to allow ample time.
