Coffee Hwiel

🔥 Over 140 Naver blog reviews

Specialty coffee roastery cafe near Sinsa Station, 12-year Blue Ribbon winner.

Coffee Hwiel is an art/theme roastery cafe located in Sinsa Garosu-gil, renowned as a specialty coffee expert and a 12-year consecutive Blue Ribbon Survey restaurant. Visitors can experience deep and rich-flavored coffee, directly roasted from carefully selected beans. The specialty coffee here is a must-try signature for any coffee enthusiast. With its gallery-like sophisticated ambiance, it's perfect for enjoying leisurely moments with friends or a partner, or for a quiet solo coffee break. Conveniently located near Sinsa Station Exit 1, it is highly recommended for those seeking high-quality coffee.
