A local favorite, this no-sign restaurant offers charcoal-grilled samgyeopsal.

Ganpan Eopneun Samgyeopsal is a local hidden gem, operating without a sign, offering fragrant charcoal-grilled samgyeopsal that draws food enthusiasts with its unique flavor. Visitors can savor the rich aroma of charcoal-grilled pork belly by the railway, experiencing a cozy and authentic atmosphere. The deeply charcoal-infused samgyeopsal is the signature dish, boasting an irresistible flavor that truly stands out. With its humble and friendly ambiance, it's perfect for enjoying a hearty meat meal with family or friends, though be prepared for potential waiting times as it's already famous among locals.
