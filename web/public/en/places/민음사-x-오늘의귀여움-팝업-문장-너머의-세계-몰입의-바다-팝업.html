Minumsa x Today's Cuteness collaboration pop-up

This pop-up, a collaboration between Minumsa and Today's Cuteness, is held on the 7th floor of Samjeong Tower in Seomyeon, Busan. The concept is 'The World Beyond Sentences: Immersion Sea,' featuring a blue, ocean-themed space where visitors can explore the opening lines of Minumsa's world literature collection.
