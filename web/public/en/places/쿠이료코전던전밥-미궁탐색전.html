An exhibition featuring Kui Ryoko's original work, Dungeon Meshi.

This exhibition explores the world of Kui Ryoko, the original author of the popular manga 'Dungeon Meshi'. It features the storyline of Dungeon Meshi Season 1 and various attractions like realistic food models, offering a unique experience for subculture fans.
