A book cafe for enjoying LPs, CDs, and reading.

Track Archive Room is a unique art/theme cafe where visitors can select and enjoy LPs and CDs while reading. Guests can freely choose from a wide range of books and music, spending their own time in a space designed for leaving personal records. The plum ade is a notable menu item, and the special experience involves selecting either a CD or LP seat to enjoy preferred music. It provides a cozy, heaven-like atmosphere for book and music lovers, highly recommended for those seeking a peaceful solo retreat or a quiet space for contemplation. Operating as a no-kids zone ensures a tranquil environment for focus, and it's advisable to check the differing weekday and weekend operating hours before visiting.
