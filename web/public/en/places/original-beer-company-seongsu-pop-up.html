Original Beer Company pop-up event in Seongsu.

The Original Beer Company Seongsu Pop-up offers a unique beer experience with a neutral tasting event in a limited-time setting. Visitors can sample various beers at the neutral tasting corner, participate in Instagram follow events for benefits, and enjoy exploring the pop-up store while receiving original tickets. The neutral tasting and original ticket collection are must-try signature experiences, with mentions of unique activities like 'monster buff.' This pop-up is perfect for friends to discover new beers and have a great time in the vibrant Seongsu area, operating from July 24 to August 13, 2026, between 11:00 and 20:00 daily.
