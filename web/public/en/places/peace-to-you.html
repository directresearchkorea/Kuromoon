A unique one-day pottery class experience in Yeonnam-dong.

Peace To You in Yeonnam-dong offers a unique one-day pottery class designed to help visitors find inner peace through ceramic art. Guests can enjoy a fulfilling sensory experience by touching and molding soft clay to create their own personalized pottery pieces. The signature activity here is crafting a one-of-a-kind cup or plate, experiencing the joy of creation firsthand. The cozy and warm workshop atmosphere is perfect for making special memories with friends or loved ones, or for enjoying a peaceful solo retreat. As popular classes may fill up quickly, it is recommended to make a reservation in advance to secure your preferred time slot.
