Jeju-themed concept dining teahouse

Yeonhwacha is a concept dining teahouse imbued with the unique mood of Jeju, offering a serene tea ceremony experience in a tranquil atmosphere. Here, solo travelers can enjoy a fulfilling time with various contents, and staff dressed in Moana-style long skirts add to the exotic ambiance. The 'Maljetmun Stone Octopus Cream Rice' paired with a unique wasabi side dish is a must-try signature menu item. This space, where one can feel Jeju's distinctive peaceful sensibility, is highly recommended for those seeking solitary contemplation or quiet relaxation.
