A unique dessert specialty store in Mullae-dong offering a wide variety of treats.

Mullae My Space is a specialty dessert shop offering a diverse world of sweets, providing visitors with a delightful culinary experience. Here, you can explore and taste an imaginative array of desserts, including various butter bars, financiers, madeleines, cakes, and Levain cookies. The five types of butter bars and the crème brûlée rare cheesecake are must-try signature items, with the yellow cheese and mint chocolate Levain cookies also being highly popular. It's an ideal spot to enjoy a sweet moment with friends or a partner in a cozy and comfortable atmosphere, or to simply indulge in a leisurely dessert time alone.
