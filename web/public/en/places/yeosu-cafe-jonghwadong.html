Retro-themed cafe in Yeosu's Jonghwadong, featured on '1 Night 2 Days' and known for its donuts.

Yeosu Cafe Jonghwadong is an art/theme cafe exuding a retro ambiance, famously featured on the popular TV show '1 Night 2 Days' with Park Bo-gum. Visitors can enjoy a delightful date in Yeosu while savoring sweet donuts and various desserts. The 'Yeosu Donut' is a must-try signature item, highly popular among those seeking delightful treats. Spanning the 2nd, 3rd floors, and a rooftop, the cafe offers a cozy and inviting atmosphere, perfect for romantic dates or gatherings with friends. Opening daily at 10:00 AM, its location near Yeosu Marine Park and Romantic Pocha makes it easily accessible.
