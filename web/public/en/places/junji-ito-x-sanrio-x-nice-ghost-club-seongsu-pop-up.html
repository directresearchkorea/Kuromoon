Unique collaboration pop-up of Junji Ito, Sanrio, and Nice Ghost Club.

This pop-up store presents an unexpectedly fresh collaboration between horror manga master Junji Ito, the adorable Sanrio characters, and the street brand Nice Ghost Club. Visitors can explore unique products where Junji Ito's signature chilling aesthetic is strangely harmonized with the cuteness of Sanrio characters. The Junji Ito x Sanrio collaboration T-shirts are particularly special items available only at this pop-up, drawing significant popularity among fans. This space is highly recommended for friends or couples who appreciate fresh and unconventional combinations that break traditional molds, offering unexpected fun.
