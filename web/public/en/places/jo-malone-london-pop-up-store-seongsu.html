Experience fragrance, multi-sensory rooms, and lucky draws at Jo Malone London Seongsu pop-up.

The Jo Malone London Seongsu pop-up offers a unique space providing diverse sensory experiences centered around fragrances. Visitors can deeply explore Jo Malone's scents through various activities like sea salt fragrance experiences, multi-sensory rooms, and scent layering. Additionally, guests can capture memories at the free four-cut photo booth and participate in a lucky draw for prizes such as sample perfume colognes. This pop-up, perfect for an indoor summer date, is ideal for friends or couples who love fragrances to create special memories together.
