A concept cafe near Seongsu Station famous for its delicious salt bread.

Beton Seongsu is a concept cafe located just a 3-minute walk from Seongsu Station Exit 4, renowned as a popular spot in Seongsu-dong, especially for its delicious salt bread. This cafe boasts an exceptional taste that rivals other famous salt bread establishments, with its salty chocolate salt bread being a signature menu item that leaves a lasting impression on visitors. It's advisable to consider potential weekend afternoon wait times when planning your visit, as it's a perfect place to enjoy freshly baked bread with coffee. For gourmands who appreciate salt bread, a visit to Beton Seongsu is highly recommended to experience its unique and special flavors.
