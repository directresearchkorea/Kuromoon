Digital drawing one-day class studio for learning folk art illustration on iPad.

Rainbow Dolphin is a unique digital drawing class studio that offers folk art illustration using iPads. Visitors can enjoy a special visual experience by drawing directly on iPads, reinterpreting traditional Korean folk art in a modern way. The signature experience here is creating beautiful folk art illustrations, which can be taken home as cherished memories. With active group classes for children's vacation experiences and youth international exchange programs, it is highly recommended for families, friends, or those seeking unique group activities.
