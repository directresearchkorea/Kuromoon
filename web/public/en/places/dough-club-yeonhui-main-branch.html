Art/theme cafe in Yeonhui-dong offering unique dough sandwiches

Dough Club Yeonhui Main Branch is an art/theme cafe located in Yeonhui-dong, offering unique dough sandwiches and a fresh culinary experience to its visitors. Here, guests can savor visually appealing dough sandwiches and enjoy a leisurely time in a sensory space. The 'special dough sandwich' stands out as a signature menu item, perfect for exploring fresh and exotic flavors. Tucked slightly away from the main street, the space creates a cozy and distinctive atmosphere, making it highly recommended for unique dates with friends or couples. It's advisable to check for potential waiting times before visiting, and note that the cafe is regularly closed every other Monday.
