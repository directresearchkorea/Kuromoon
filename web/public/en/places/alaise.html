French fine dining restaurant in Hannam-dong, listed in the Michelin Guide.

AL'AISE, located in Hannam-dong, is a French fine dining restaurant listed in the Michelin Guide, offering a special gastronomic experience through its luxurious course meals. The restaurant's exterior builds anticipation, and guests can savor AL'AISE's unique Western cuisine through lunch courses at ₩85,000 and dinner courses at ₩170,000. Its elegant ambiance makes it ideal for celebrating anniversaries or enjoying special dates with loved ones, and private rooms are available for more intimate gatherings. While conveniently located near Hannam Station on the Gyeongui-Jungang Line, it's advisable to consider the irregular train schedules when planning your visit by public transport.
