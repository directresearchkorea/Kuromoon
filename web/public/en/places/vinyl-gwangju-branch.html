An emotional listening cafe to enjoy LP music.

Vinyl Gwangju Branch is a unique listening cafe where visitors can immerse themselves in analog sensibilities while enjoying LP music. Guests can purchase a vinyl single-person admission ticket at the 2nd-floor counter, offering a special experience of diving into their own musical world. This cafe provides deep relaxation and emotion through LP music, focusing on the listening experience itself rather than specific signature menus. Its tastefully emotional interior makes it a perfect spot for couples on a date, friends, or those seeking a quiet moment alone.
