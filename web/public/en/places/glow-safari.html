A media art themed kids cafe, perfect for families with children.

Glow Safari is a unique kids cafe combining media art with a safari theme. During hot summer days, families can enjoy a special time indoors, experiencing fantastic visual displays reminiscent of fireflies. The safari-themed space particularly stimulates children's imagination, with various media art contents adding to the immersive experience. This cafe is highly recommended for families with young children seeking an engaging indoor activity, especially during school holidays.
