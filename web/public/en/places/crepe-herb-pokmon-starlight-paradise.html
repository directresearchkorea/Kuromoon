Pokémon-themed pop-up store at Lotte World Mall, Jamsil.

This pop-up store presents a fantastical atmosphere with its unique 'Pokémon Starlight Paradise' theme, featuring a giant Poké Ball mapping and a Gyarados sculpture. Visitors can enjoy collaborative food items from brands like Sadler Haus, Crepe Herb, and Matt Black, along with capsule toys (Gacha), experiencing diverse Pokémon content. The Pichu and Pikachu character crepes are a must-try signature menu, and visitors can receive a Magikarp card with pre-booking or a sun cap by following on social media. Set in an outdoor two-story container space, this experience is perfect for friends, couples, or families who love Pokémon, offering special memories. Plan a richer visit by taking advantage of pre-booking benefits and participating in social media events.
