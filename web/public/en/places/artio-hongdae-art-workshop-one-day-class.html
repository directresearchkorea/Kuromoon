Unique one-day art class experience in Hongdae, Seoul.

Artio Hongdae Art Workshop One-Day Class offers a unique drawing and art experience in Hongdae, providing an distinctive space for creative activities. Visitors can enjoy visual and sensory pleasures by creating their own artworks through various art activities, including silk fan making and watercolor classes. The silk fan making, in particular, is a signature experience here, perfect for creating special memories and souvenirs. This cozy and creative workshop is recommended for unique dates with partners, fun times with friends, as well as corporate workshops or group cultural experiences. With programs also available for foreign students, it offers an attractive experience to a wider range of visitors.
