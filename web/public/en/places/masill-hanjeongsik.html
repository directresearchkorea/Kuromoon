Masill Hanjeongsik in Cheongju, a Korean dining restaurant perfect for family gatherings and formal meetings.

Masill Hanjeongsik in Cheongju's Seonghwa-dong is a fusion Korean dining restaurant specializing in private room dining, ideal for important occasions like formal family meetings (sanggyeonnye) or family gatherings. Guests can enjoy a private dining experience in individual rooms, savoring refined and elegant Korean course meals. While no specific signature dish is mentioned, the fusion Korean course meal itself is the highlight of the experience. The clean and quiet ambiance makes it perfect for spending intimate time with loved ones, especially recommended for formal family meetings, meals with parents, or group gatherings. The restaurant offers ample parking and accommodates reservations and group bookings, so it's advisable to make a reservation before visiting.
