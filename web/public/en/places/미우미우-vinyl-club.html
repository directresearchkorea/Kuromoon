Miu Miu's Vinyl Club pop-up store

The Miu Miu Vinyl Club pop-up at The Hyundai Seoul reinterprets the sensibility of a music collector's record library through Miu Miu's unique perspective. Visitors can explore turntables, records, and Marshall × Miu Miu headphones and speakers, alongside the 26 FW new collection.
