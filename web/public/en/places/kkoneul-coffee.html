Kkoneul Coffee

Newly opened pet-friendly cafe near Sangbong Station, known for delicious desserts.

Kkoneul Coffee is a newly opened art/theme cafe located between Sangbong and Jungnang Stations, filled with the delightful aroma of desserts. Visitors can enjoy various desserts and spend comfortable time with their pets, with daily changing menus often announced on Instagram. Known as a dessert hotspot, guests should definitely try the fragrant and delicious desserts offered here. The cozy and warm ambiance makes it perfect for creating special memories with friends, partners, or pets, highly recommended for those seeking a new cafe experience. Parking for one car is available, and the cafe is closed on Sundays, so it's advisable to check opening hours before visiting.
