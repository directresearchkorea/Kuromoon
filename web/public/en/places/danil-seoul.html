Newly opened cafe in Seongsu with a simple and minimalist vibe.

Danil Seoul is a newly opened art/theme cafe in Seongsu-dong, focusing on simplicity and the inherent flavor of coffee beans rather than elaborate decor. Visitors can enjoy high-quality coffee in a clean, minimalist space, offering a tranquil escape from the bustling city. The '33 BLEND Flat White' is a must-try signature menu item, highly recommended for those who appreciate a rich and deep coffee flavor. Its calm and understated interior, distinct from the typical Seongsu vibe, makes it ideal for solo visitors seeking quiet time or friends and couples desiring a peaceful conversation. While many Seongsu cafes open late, Danil Seoul opens at 10:00 AM, making it a great option for early morning visits.
