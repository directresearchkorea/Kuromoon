EGA Brain Sauna

🔥 Over 2 Naver blog reviews
✅ Recent status: 운영 중

A brain recovery sauna based on reverse aging research, offering a wellness retreat.

EGA Brain Sauna is a unique wellness space focused on brain recovery, developed based on Reverse Aging research. Visitors can experience a special sauna session designed for brain restoration, fostering a sense of emptiness and enhanced by multi-layered recovery scents curated by Scentwalk for an immersive spatial experience. The brain recovery sauna itself is the signature offering, with the specially designed ambient fragrances playing a crucial role in creating the overall therapeutic atmosphere. This serene environment is ideal for those seeking mental and physical rest, making it a perfect indoor date spot for couples or friends, especially during hot summer days. Opportunities for free trials might be available through Woonas' Instagram, and reservations can be conveniently made via Naver Booking.
