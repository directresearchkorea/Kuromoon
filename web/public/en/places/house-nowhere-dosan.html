A popup/exhibition space in Dosan featuring Gentle Monster and Nudake.

House Nowhere Dosan is a sensory space where Gentle Monster and Nudake collaborate to present a unique visual strategy combining dessert and fashion. Visitors can experience Gentle Monster's artistic spatial design while enjoying Nudake's visually striking desserts, making for a special visit. The White Truffle Cake is a particularly famous and original dessert, and the architectural beauty of the space itself serves as an excellent photo zone. It is ideal for friends or couples who appreciate fashion and unique desserts, offering a stylish environment for shared experiences. Valet parking is available, and the venue operates daily from 11:00 to 21:00, which is helpful to note when planning a visit.
