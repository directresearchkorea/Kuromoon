Premium hotpot restaurant known for excellent service and delicious food.

Haidilao is a premium Chinese hotpot restaurant renowned for its exceptional service and fresh ingredients. Visitors can customize their hotpot experience by selecting from a variety of broths, fresh seafood, meats, and vegetables, while the spectacular noodle dance performed at the table adds visual entertainment. The numbing spicy Mala soup and the rich tomato soup are must-try signature broths, complemented by the fun of creating personalized dipping sauces at the self-service bar. Its vibrant and clean interior makes it ideal for gatherings with friends, couples, or families, highly recommended for anyone seeking a unique culinary adventure. Due to its popularity, especially on weekends or evenings, it's advisable to make reservations in advance or be prepared for a wait.
