Ring Workshop Atelier Hosu Hongdae

A ring-making workshop cafe in Hongdae, perfect for couples.

Atelier Hosu Hongdae is a unique concept cafe and ring workshop located in Hongdae, Mapo-gu, Seoul. It offers a special experience where couples or friends can design and create their own one-of-a-kind couple rings or friendship rings, making it perfect for creating cherished memories. This spot is highly recommended as a date course in Hongdae; after crafting your rings, you can enjoy the vibrant atmosphere, diverse restaurants, and attractions of the Hongdae area for a richer day out. The rings you make yourself become meaningful souvenirs, more than just accessories.
