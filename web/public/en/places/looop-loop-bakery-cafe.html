Vegan bakery cafe in Seongsu-dong, famous for its unique Agjak Cake.

LOOOP Loop Bakery Cafe, located in Seongdong-gu, Seoul, is an ideal spot for those seeking healthy and delicious vegan desserts. This cafe offers a variety of vegan baked goods alongside coffee, beverages, and wine, with neatly presented, small-sized desserts that are visually appealing. It is highly recommended for dessert lovers to experience the 'Agjak Cake,' frequently featured in mukbangs and sparking curiosity, making it a perfect destination during a visit to Seongsu-dong. Beyond its signature Agjak Cake, a diverse selection of vegan desserts is available, promising a unique culinary experience.
