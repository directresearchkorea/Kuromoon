Mandori Shilla Stay Gwanghwamun

🔥 Over 117 Naver blog reviews
✅ Recent status: 운영 중

A popular Dakdoritang (spicy braised chicken) restaurant near Gwanghwamun and Jonggak Station.

Mandori Shilla Stay Gwanghwamun is a highly accessible restaurant specializing in Dakdoritang, located near Gwanghwamun and Jonggak stations. Situated in an alley opposite Shilla Stay Gwanghwamun, it's an ideal spot for a meal after visiting the National Museum of Modern and Contemporary Art or Sejong Center. The signature dishes here are the spicy and hearty Dakdoritang and Dakbokkeumtang, known for their rich flavors. This restaurant is highly recommended for company dinners, group gatherings, or an evening meal with friends in the Gwanghwamun and Jonggak area. Its convenient location within walking distance from Jonggak, Anguk, and Gwanghwamun subway stations makes it easy to visit.
