A bright and clean cake cafe near Mullae Station, known for delicious cakes.

Chef Jo Mullae Main Branch is an art/theme cafe boasting a bright and clean atmosphere with a modern white-toned interior. Visitors can comfortably enjoy a wide variety of cakes in the spacious seating area, with mango and strawberry cakes being particularly popular. The mango cake, featured on 'Life Master' (생활의 달인), is Chef Jo's signature item, offering an exquisite blend of fresh mango and soft cream. Its modern and neat white-toned space is perfect for gatherings with friends or romantic dates, highly recommended for anyone who loves sweet desserts. Cakes can be purchased without a reservation, and the cafe is conveniently located near Mullae Station.
