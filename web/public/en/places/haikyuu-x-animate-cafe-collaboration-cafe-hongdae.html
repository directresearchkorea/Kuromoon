Haikyuu!! collaboration pop-up cafe with limited goods and drinks.

This collaboration cafe brings together the popular anime 'Haikyuu!!' and Animate Cafe, offering a unique experience for fans. Visitors can enjoy collaboration drinks themed after the characters and participate in an event to receive Korea-exclusive photo cards. Collaboration drinks are priced at ₩7,000, and purchases over ₩30,000 grant one random Korea-exclusive photo card (20 types available). Designed for anime enthusiasts, it's an ideal spot for friends or couples to create memories with their favorite characters. Given its popularity, attracting many visitors on its opening day, it's advisable to plan your visit accordingly.
