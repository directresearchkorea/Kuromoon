Sungsimdang's cake specialty shop in Daejeon, offering a variety of desserts.

Sungsimdang Cake Boutique is a specialized shop offering fresh and diverse seasonal cakes and desserts from Sungsimdang. Visitors can enjoy the pleasure of choosing from a wide array of cakes, breads, and sandwiches made with Sungsimdang's unique recipes. Limited edition seasonal items like the Fig Siru Cake are a must-try signature dessert. With its warm and lively atmosphere, it's perfect for enjoying a sweet time with family, friends, or loved ones, or as a stop on a Daejeon bakery tour. Expect potential waiting lines on weekends, and linked parking lots offer one hour of free parking per receipt.
