Cheongju's official Gentle Monster retailer, an eyewear store with style and expertise.

Jeen House is an eyewear store in Cheongju known for combining style with professional expertise, notably as an official retailer for Gentle Monster. Visitors can try on a wide range of eyeglasses and sunglasses, receiving expert consultations to find products that best suit them. Gentle Monster products are a signature offering, making it a popular destination for those seeking eyewear in the Bokdae-dong area. The store offers a sophisticated and professional atmosphere for comfortable eyewear shopping, ideal for fashion-conscious friends or couples.
