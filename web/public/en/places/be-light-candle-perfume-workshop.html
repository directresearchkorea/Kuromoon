A workshop in Yongsan to create your own perfumes and candles.

Be Light Candle & Perfume Workshop is a place that offers unique and emotional hands-on experiences, aiming to infuse the world with beautiful scents. Here, visitors can create their own fragrant masterpieces through various one-day classes, such as making room sprays, which have been popular with students from Huam Elementary School during summer vacation. Guests can blend their own perfumes or craft candles, creating one-of-a-kind signature items and cherishing special memories. The emotionally resonant space is perfect for enjoying a unique experience with friends, couples, or family, or for finding healing through solo time. As there are many group inquiries from schools and organizations, it is advisable to contact them in advance to book your desired class.
