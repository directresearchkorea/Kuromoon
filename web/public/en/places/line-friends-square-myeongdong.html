LINE FRIENDS Square Myeongdong

LINE FRIENDS character shop in Myeongdong, offering various attractions including Sylvanian Families.

LINE FRIENDS Square Myeongdong is a multi-cultural space where visitors can encounter LINE FRIENDS characters and various collaboration products. Guests can enjoy a diverse shopping experience, exploring not only LINE FRIENDS character merchandise like BTS and Kedeheon but also the Sylvanian Families store. Must-see attractions include rare goods such as Sylvanian random boxes and 40th-anniversary commemorative items. Conveniently located in front of Myeongdong Arts Theater, it's an ideal spot for friends or families to visit, browse character products, and create memories. While no specific reservation or waiting information is mentioned, it's a worthwhile destination when visiting Myeongdong.
