Enjoy books and relaxation at the newly renovated Daegu Dongbu Library.

Daegu Metropolitan Dongbu Library has been newly renovated to welcome citizens as a complex cultural space, offering a cafe-like visual appeal and relaxation in addition to its traditional functions of reading and borrowing books. The 'Library of Contemplation' is highlighted as the most splendid area within the library, where visitors can enjoy leisurely time with books. With its sophisticated, cafe-like ambiance, it is recommended for friends, couples, or anyone seeking a quiet time alone to enjoy books. Free parking is available directly in front of the building, making it convenient for visitors.
