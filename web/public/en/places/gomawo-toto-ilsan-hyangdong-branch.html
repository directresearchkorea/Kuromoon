Kids cooking class and sensory play experience space for children.

Gomawo Toto Ilsan Hyangdong Branch offers unique kids' cooking classes and sensory play experiences designed to help children become familiar with vegetables and stimulate their five senses. The diverse cooking classes utilizing various vegetables are a signature activity, helping children develop healthy eating habits. Set in a bright and safe environment, this place is highly recommended for families looking to create special memories with their children.
