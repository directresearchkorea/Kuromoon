A healthy gimbap restaurant known as one of Seoul's top 3 gimbap spots.

Suadang Seongbuk Branch is renowned as one of Seoul's top three gimbap restaurants, offering unique and healthy gimbap options. Its distinctive feature is the chewy yet crispy gimbap wrap made from rice flour, providing a texture that sets it apart from typical gimbap. It's an ideal spot for solo diners, operating daily from early morning to evening, making it convenient for anyone seeking a healthy and satisfying meal. The signature Dubai Gimbap, priced at ₩19,900 for 10 pieces, is particularly famous for its unique flavor and texture, attracting many loyal customers.
