XYZ Suglow (Seongsu)

🗓️ Period: 2026-08-01 ~ 2026-08-28
🔥 Over 3 Naver blog reviews
✅ Recent status: 행사 중

A pop-up store in Seongsu's XYZ building featuring low-sugar desserts.

Suglow, a renowned low-sugar dessert cafe from Jamsil, has opened a pop-up store at the XYZ building in Seongsu, offering healthy dessert options. Visitors can indulge in a variety of low-sugar desserts, including the popular Peach Earl Grey Pudding, enjoying a unique culinary experience. The low-sugar dessert menu, especially items like the Peach Earl Grey Pudding, are highly recommended as the pop-up's signature offerings, balancing sweetness with health. Located in the trendy Yeonmujang-gil area of Seongsu, this pop-up is perfect for friends or couples seeking healthy yet delicious treats in a stylish setting. While no specific reservation details are provided, its convenient location near Seongsu Station Exit 4 makes it easily accessible for a delightful visit during its month-long run.
