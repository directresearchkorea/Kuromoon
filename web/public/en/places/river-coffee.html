A cafe offering latte art one-day classes.

River Coffee is a unique space where visitors can directly experience the artistic side of coffee through latte art one-day classes. Under the friendly guidance of professional baristas, guests can create their own beautiful latte art, enjoying both visual pleasure and a sense of accomplishment. The self-made latte art coffee serves as a signature experience and a perfect photo zone to capture precious memories. With its cozy and comfortable atmosphere, it's an ideal spot for friends or couples seeking a unique date, or for individuals looking to enjoy a special hobby time. Advance reservations are essential for the one-day classes, so it is recommended to inquire for detailed information before visiting.
