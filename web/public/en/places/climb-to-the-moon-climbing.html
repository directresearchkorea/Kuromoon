A climbing center in Songpa-gu, offering unique bouldering experiences.

Climb to the Moon Climbing is a leading climbing center in Songpa-gu, specializing in natural rock bouldering and dynamic movement training, offering a unique experiential space. Through collaboration with Cliffstay, it provides various climbing experiences, including expeditions to Jukdoam, and even first-timers can safely learn climbing with national team partners. The core content here is dynamic movement training, a signature program that helps improve skills through technical approaches beyond simple jumping. With professional instruction and a systematic training system, it is recommended for friends or couples new to climbing, as well as experienced climbers looking to enhance their abilities, and for a more effective first visit, it is advisable to come with a professional partner rather than alone.
