Sutgarak Bansang Masil

🔥 Over 5119 Naver blog reviews

A clean fusion Korean course meal restaurant in Nowon.

Sutgarak Bansang Masil is a Korean restaurant in Nowon specializing in fusion Korean course meals, ideal for family gatherings and formal meetings like 'sanggyeonrye' (pre-marriage family meetings). Visitors can leisurely enjoy diverse seasonal course dishes, and the establishment offers both private rooms and a main hall, allowing for various types of gatherings. Signature options include the Masil Jeongshik, Basic Jeongshik, and Special Jeongshik, providing a range of course meals to suit different preferences. Despite the building's older exterior, the interior is neatly maintained, offering a clean and formal atmosphere perfect for making special memories with loved ones. It's advisable to check their operating hours before visiting, especially for the weekday break time from 15:00 to 17:00.
