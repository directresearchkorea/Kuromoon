BIGBANG 20th anniversary official light stick pop-up

This special pop-up store celebrates BIGBANG's 20th anniversary, showcasing official light sticks designed to encapsulate their 20-year musical journey. Visitors can experience the 20th-anniversary edition light stick within a theme where 'XX' signifies BIGBANG's 20 years and 'COSMOS' represents BIGBANG and V.I.P. The 20th-anniversary edition official light stick, characterized by its design depicting BIGBANG's musical journey on a staff, is a must-have signature merchandise item. It's a special place for K-POP fandom, especially V.I.P, BIGBANG's long-time fans, to visit with friends, share memories, and commemorate the occasion. As it operates simultaneously in both Myeongdong and Seongsu, it is advisable to check the location before visiting.
