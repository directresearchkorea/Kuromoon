Balcony Garden

✅ Recent status: 운영 중

A cafe near Hongdae Station, famous for its peach and mango half-and-half bingsu and cookie classes.

Balcony Garden is a cozy cafe located near Hongdae Station, offering a delightful selection of sweet desserts and beverages. Visitors can enjoy a refreshing and sweet experience, especially with their half-and-half peach and mango bingsu packed with fresh fruits during summer, and cookie classes are also available. The signature item is this half-and-half bingsu, allowing you to savor both tangy peach and sweet mango simultaneously. With its comfortable and relaxed atmosphere, it's an ideal spot for friends or couples to enjoy a sweet time together, or for individuals seeking a peaceful break. Based on a Sunday evening visit with no waiting, it appears to be a place where you can generally visit without long queues.
