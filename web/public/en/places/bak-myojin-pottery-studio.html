Pottery studio in Dongnae-gu, Busan, offering ceramic experiences.

Bak Myojin Pottery Studio, located in Dongnae-gu, Busan, is a specialized pottery experience workshop where everyone can directly experience ceramic art and enjoy the pleasure of creation. In a spacious and comfortable environment, visitors can craft their own unique pieces through various methods like wheel throwing or hand-building, later receiving their finished pottery after glazing and firing. The signature programs include wheel throwing (₩50,000) and hand-building (₩35,000 per person), which are particularly popular among families as elementary school students can easily participate. With a cozy atmosphere showcasing carefully crafted ceramics, it's an ideal place for friends, couples, or families to create special memories and foster children's creativity. For class and experience inquiries, it is recommended to check detailed information and make reservations through the studio's blog.
