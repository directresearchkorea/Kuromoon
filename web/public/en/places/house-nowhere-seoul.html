Gentle Monster's Seongsu popup/exhibition space.

House Nowhere Seoul is a popup/exhibition space showcasing Gentle Monster's unique worldview and artistic sensibility. Visitors can enjoy visual delights on the first floor, where Gentle Monster products are displayed alongside peculiar vegetable-like doll objects. The experimental designs and artistic installations characteristic of Gentle Monster offer a signature experience unique to this venue. It is recommended for friends or couples seeking a special visual experience in Seongsu-dong, appealing to those interested in fashion and art. Parking is available for car visitors, with a 2 hours free parking benefit upon product purchase.
