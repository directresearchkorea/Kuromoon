Tojours Bread Stone Kiln Village, a famous bakery pilgrimage spot in Cheonan.

Tojours Bread Stone Kiln Village in Cheonan is a renowned bakery pilgrimage destination, operating as a unique concept cafe where different types of bread are sold in various buildings. It is particularly famous for its diverse range of breads, including stone kiln-baked manju and the signature turtle bread, boasting exceptional taste and quality that ranks it among the top bakeries nationwide. Visitors can find the widest selection of breads at the main bakery hall, and due to its popularity, it's advisable to plan your visit to avoid long queues. For those seeking fresh and delicious bread, Tojours Bread Stone Kiln Village is a highly recommended spot in Cheonan.
