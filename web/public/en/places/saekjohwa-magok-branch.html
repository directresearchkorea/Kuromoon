A unique drawing cafe near Magok Station, perfect for indoor dates.

Saekjohwa Magok Branch, conveniently located right outside Magok Station Exit 3, is a unique indoor drawing cafe perfect for escaping the heat. Visitors can engage in various artistic activities, including backdrop painting, making hand mirrors, and creating pin badges, to craft their own unique masterpieces. Backdrop painting stands out as a signature activity, offering the joy of expressing creativity and completing a personalized artwork. The sparkling rainbow-colored interior creates a bright and lively atmosphere, making it an ideal spot for unique indoor dates for couples or for friends to create fun memories. Its excellent accessibility from Magok Station makes it easy to find and a comfortable place to visit for artistic inspiration.
