Hanamikoji handmade shoes & casual brands pop-up store

The Hanamikoji Pop-up Store is a special event where visitors can discover various casual style brands, including the handmade shoe specialist Hanamikoji, alongside Coolkitten and Citybreeze. Guests can try on and purchase trendy women's shoes such as Mary Jane shoes, canvas shoes, and mules, while enjoying browsing the unique objects and products from each brand. Signature items include Hanamikoji's meticulously crafted handmade shoes, often available with special discounts exclusive to the pop-up, making it an ideal place to find unique pieces to complete one's style. This pop-up is perfect for those interested in stylish casual shoes and fashion items, offering a delightful shopping experience for friends or couples to compare products from multiple brands in one convenient location.
