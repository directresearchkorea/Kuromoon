An art workshop in Hyehwa offering unique experiences like Bearbrick painting.

Kkeujeok Kkeujeok Art Workshop in Hyehwa (Daehangno) is a unique art experience space designed for visitors to create their own artworks and enjoy a delightful time. The Bearbrick painting experience is particularly popular, offering a creative and sensory activity for those seeking new things to do in Hyehwa beyond theater. Here, you can design and color your own personalized Bearbrick, creating special memories and a cherished souvenir. With a cozy and comfortable atmosphere, it's perfect for unique dates with friends or partners, or for solo visitors looking to find artistic inspiration. As it can get crowded on weekends, it is recommended to plan your visit in advance.
