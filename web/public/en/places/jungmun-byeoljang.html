Art/theme dessert omakase cafe in Seogwipo, Jeju.

Jungmun Byeoljang is an art/theme cafe located in Seogwipo, Jeju, presenting a special dessert omakase under a luxurious villa concept. Visitors can enjoy both gourmet dining and relaxation while admiring the beautiful ocean view and surrounding natural landscapes like Daepo Jusangjeolli Cliff and Cheonjeyeon Waterfall. The signature dessert omakase, with its artistic plating and unique flavors, is a must-try experience that satisfies all senses. The luxurious villa-like interior creates an ideal setting for romantic dates with loved ones, special gatherings with friends, or simply enjoying some peaceful alone time.
