A multi-cultural complex in Gwangalli, Busan (cafe, bookstore, gallery)

Podium Dive, a multi-cultural complex located in Gwangalli, Busan, offers diverse cultural experiences with an art shop and cafe on B1, a gallery on B2, and a bookstore (Arc N Book) on B3. It's an ideal indoor space to enjoy comfortably even on hot days, providing visitors with a variety of pleasures by combining a cafe, bookstore, and gallery all in one place. Particularly popular among the MZ generation, it features unique items like the 'Kim Mozzi' doll and other trending items seen on Reels, making it highly recommended for those seeking special entertainment in Gwangalli. It's an attractive venue where you can simultaneously enjoy a stylish art shop, leisurely reading, appreciating art, and delicious beverages, satisfying all your senses.
