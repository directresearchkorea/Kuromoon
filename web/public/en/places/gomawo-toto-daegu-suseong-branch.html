Gomawo Toto Daegu Suseong Branch

🔥 Over 220 Naver blog reviews
✅ Recent status: 운영 중

A hands-on sensory play cafe where children can enjoy soil-based activities.

Gomawo Toto Daegu Suseong Branch is a unique experiential kids' cafe where children can directly touch and feel natural soil, promoting sensory development. Here, children engage in various tactile play activities with soil, fostering creativity and emotional stability while enjoying free exploration in a safe and hygienic environment. The core content is the soil sensory play, offering a special experience where children interact with nature by touching and exploring the earth themselves. The space is designed with a nature-friendly atmosphere, allowing children to play freely, making it highly recommended for families with young children up to early elementary school age. Operating on a 100% reservation basis, it's essential to complete your booking before visiting, and while parking is available behind the building, only two spots are accessible on weekdays.
