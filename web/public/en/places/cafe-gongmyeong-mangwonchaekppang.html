A book cafe in Mangwon-dong offering books and bakery items.

Cafe Gongmyeong Mangwonchaekppang, operated by the publisher Film Inc., is a bakery cafe offering an emotional space filled with books. Spanning from the basement to the second floor, the cafe features numerous books displayed throughout and fresh bread in the center, providing a delightful visual experience. Various bakery desserts, including biscuit choux, are highly praised, making them must-try items during your visit. The ambiance is perfect for quiet work or reading with books, and it's also suitable as a date spot in Mangwon, recommended for both solo visits and couples.
