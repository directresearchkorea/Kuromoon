Hyundai Card Alphabet Card pop-up in Hannam-dong offering unique design and brand experience.

The Hyundai Card Alphabet Card pop-up, launched as an independent brand after 20 years, offers a unique brand experience that goes beyond a simple card, utilizing alphabet design elements. Visitors can enjoy a special experience from the moment of unboxing, thanks to its vertical design and metal plate packaging, and directly engage with various alphabet-themed design content. The pop-up's unique design and alphabet-infused space are its signature, allowing for a deeper brand experience through related items like keycaps. This experience is highly recommended for those seeking something distinctive beyond a mere card introduction, making it ideal for visiting with friends or partners interested in design and brand storytelling.
