Art-themed cookie cafe in Jeju Dongmun Market

Charlie Factory Jeju Dongmun Market Main Branch is an art-themed cafe located in Jeju's Dongmun Market, renowned for its 'Dubai Chewy Cookies'. Visitors can enjoy a unique ambiance while tasting various chewy cookies or purchasing them as souvenirs from their Jeju trip. The signature Dubai Chewy Cookies are a must-try delicacy, and the charming interior also serves as an excellent photo zone. It's a perfect spot for friends, couples, or families to enjoy a sweet break during their visit to Dongmun Market, with convenient access to the Dongmun Traditional Market public parking lot.
