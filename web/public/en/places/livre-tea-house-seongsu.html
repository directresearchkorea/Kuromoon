Unique one-day class in Seongsu to create your own tea.

Livre Tea House Seongsu offers a unique one-day class where visitors can create their own special tea, providing a space to express oneself and experience something new through tea. With neatly arranged materials and a tablet, participants can follow a structured class, enjoying the pleasure of combining various ingredients to complete their own unique blended tea. The signature experience here is creating 'your own tea,' which goes beyond a simple beverage to become a cherished souvenir filled with special memories and meaning. Held in a calm and organized atmosphere, this class is perfect for a unique date course with a loved one, creating special memories with friends, or enjoying focused solo time.
