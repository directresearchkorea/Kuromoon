Live jazz bar located on Hangang-daero in Yongsan.

Awesome Ground Jazz Bar, located on Hangang-daero in Yongsan, offers a unique experience with live jazz performances every Friday. Guests can enjoy the subtle melodies of the night along with various drinks like Cheetah Highball and Hoegaarden beer. The venue provides amenities such as group seating, wireless internet, and separate restrooms for men and women.
