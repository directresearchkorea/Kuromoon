A representative bindaetteok restaurant in Gwangjang Market

Sunheene Bindaetteok is a renowned bindaetteok restaurant in Seoul's Gwangjang Market, famous for its freshly made, savory, and crispy bindaetteok every day. Beyond bindaetteok, it's a beloved spot where food enthusiasts can enjoy various Gwangjang Market delicacies like yukhoe (beef tartare) and glutinous rice twisted donuts. Its excellent accessibility from Jongno 5-ga Station makes it convenient to visit, and it's also great for a stroll along Cheonggyecheon Stream after a meal. Many recommend pairing the crispy bindaetteok with makgeolli (rice wine) for an authentic experience, making it a must-visit for those seeking to savor traditional Korean flavors amidst the vibrant atmosphere of Gwangjang Market.
