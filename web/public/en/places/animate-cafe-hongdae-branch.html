A themed cafe to find Digimon collaboration merchandise.

Animate Cafe Hongdae is a themed cafe offering an unforgettable experience to fans through its special collaboration with the popular anime 'Digimon Adventure'. Visitors can explore and purchase various merchandise in a space decorated with a Digimon Adventure theme, enjoying the feeling of directly experiencing the world of the series. Especially, the limited-time Digimon-themed special menus and exclusive merchandise are considered must-have items for any fan. Located on the 5th floor of AK Mall Hongdae, it provides a cool and pleasant environment, perfect for anime-loving friends, couples, or solo fans to enjoy their hobby, offering various attractions alongside other kidult shops. Advance reservations may be required for weekend visits, so it's advisable to check before visiting.
