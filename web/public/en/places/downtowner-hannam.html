Hannam-dong's popular handmade burger spot, equipped with high chairs.

Downtowner Hannam is a renowned handmade burger restaurant located in Hannam-dong, Yongsan-gu, Seoul, celebrated for its diverse range of fresh, handmade burgers and crispy onion rings. It's an excellent spot for a date in Hannam-dong and is conveniently located near the Hannam-dong public parking lot, ensuring easy access for visitors. The establishment is also family-friendly, offering high chairs for children, and its set menu, which includes onion rings, a cup salad, and a soda, is a highly popular and value-for-money option among patrons.
