LG Electronics Brand Exhibition - THE FIRST : Origins

🔥 Over 3 Naver blog reviews

LG Electronics' media art pop-up exhibition showcasing its history.

LG Electronics' brand exhibition, 'THE FIRST : Origins,' is a unique pop-up store that tells the story of the company's founding and its rise as a leading electronics firm through captivating media art. Visitors can visually and sensually experience LG Electronics' technological prowess and brand story through surprisingly impressive media art, even if they visit without high expectations. The media art itself is the must-see signature content, allowing guests to fully appreciate the harmony of technology and art. Located near Apgujeong Rodeo in Gangnam, it is highly recommended for friends or couples seeking a distinctive activity and those interested in the intersection of technology and art.
