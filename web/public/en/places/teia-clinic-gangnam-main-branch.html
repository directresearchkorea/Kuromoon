Gangnam Station dermatology clinic offering AI skin diagnosis and customized treatments.

Teia Clinic Gangnam Main Branch is a specialized dermatology clinic that utilizes an AI skin diagnosis system to provide personalized, optimized skin solutions. Visitors can receive a precise analysis of their skin condition through the AI system, then effectively address their concerns with various treatments such as Juvélux Skin or Onda Lifting. The AI diagnostic system offers a unique and differentiated experience, with Juvélux Skin and Onda Lifting being popular signature treatments sought by many. Recommended for brides-to-be or anyone interested in consistent skin care within a systematic and professional medical environment, it boasts excellent accessibility, just a 3-minute walk from Gangnam Station. Operating year-round for convenient visits, the clinic also offers paid parking within the building, making it accessible for those arriving by car.
