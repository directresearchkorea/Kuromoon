A healing hanok cafe in Ikseon-dong with rain sounds and photo zones.

Rain Report Rainbow, nestled in the heart of Ikseon-dong alley, is a unique hanok cafe offering a distinctive concept where visitors can relax to the soothing sound of rain. Guests can immerse themselves in the tranquil hanok ambiance, enjoying the sight and sound of falling rain, while capturing memorable photos at various designated photo zones. The cafe boasts visually stunning dessert menus that delight both the eyes and palate, with its numerous photo spots being a must-visit signature feature. Open year-round, it's perfect for a casual visit and highly recommended for friends exploring Ikseon-dong or couples seeking a special date experience.
