Hae-bangchon's LP cafe & bar, offering music, cocktails, and pudding.

Hills & Europa in Haebangchon is an atmospheric LP cafe and bar, offering a tranquil escape to heal with music away from daily complexities. Visitors can enjoy a wide selection of LP music and spend relaxing moments, potentially enhanced by special exhibitions that occasionally take place. The signature pudding and uniquely named cocktails like 'Cigar in a Glass' and 'Grinch' are highly recommended to try. With its cozy Haebangchon ambiance and flowing LP music, this space is perfect for dates, gatherings with friends, or quiet solo reflection.
