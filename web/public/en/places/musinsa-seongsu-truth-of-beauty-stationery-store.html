A stationery-themed pop-up in Seongsu for beauty product experiences and photos.

Musinsa Seongsu Truth of Beauty (T.O.B.) Starlight Stationery Store is a unique pop-up where visitors can experience beauty products and take photos in a stationery store concept. Guests can try various beauty products in a starry, emotional atmosphere and participate in special activities like the 'Trace Eraser' experience. The 'Trace Eraser' experience offers a unique memory, and various photo zones throughout the space are perfect for capturing memorable moments. With its nostalgic stationery vibe, it's recommended for friends or couples looking for an unusual date spot, or anyone wanting to add a special experience to their Seongsu outing. Located at Musinsa Beauty Space 1, it's easily accessible near Musinsa Standard.
