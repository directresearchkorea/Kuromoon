LG Twins Goodbye Jamsil pop-up exhibition featuring commemorative merchandise.

The LG Twins Goodbye Jamsil commemorative merchandise pop-up exhibition is held next to the central ticket office at Jamsil Baseball Stadium. It operates during a three-game series that includes the October 4th KIA game, offering various LG Twins related commemorative items.
