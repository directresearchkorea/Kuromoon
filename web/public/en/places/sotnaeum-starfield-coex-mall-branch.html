COEX restaurant specializing in pot rice, great for solo diners.

Sotnaeum Starfield COEX Mall Branch is a pot rice specialty restaurant located in the basement of COEX Mall near Samseong Station, where you can enjoy warm pot rice meticulously prepared with fresh ingredients. It offers a variety of menus, including the 1-person assorted seafood pot rice and limited octopus pot rice set, making it a great option for solo diners seeking a hearty meal. This place is highly recommended for those looking for a clean and satisfying meal in a neat atmosphere, and it's also a good choice for anyone wanting a moment of peace in their busy daily life. It operates daily from 10:30 to 22:00, with a break time from 15:00 to 16:00 and a last order at 21:00, so it's good to keep this in mind when visiting.
