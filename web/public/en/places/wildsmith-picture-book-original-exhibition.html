A large-scale retrospective exhibition of picture book master Brian Wildsmith at Seoul Arts Center.

The 'Wildsmith Picture Book Original Exhibition' held at the Seoul Calligraphy Art Museum in Seoul Arts Center is a large-scale retrospective of the world-renowned picture book master, Brian Wildsmith. This exhibition showcases over 200 original artworks featuring his unique and vibrant color palettes, modern media art, and interactive activity spaces. In particular, visitors are captivated by various art goods and photo zones that allow them to dive deeper into his creative world. With its warm illustrative style and colorful tones that stimulate imagination, it is highly recommended for families, couples, and design enthusiasts alike. Since weekends tend to get crowded, booking your tickets in advance is recommended to ensure a smooth entry.
