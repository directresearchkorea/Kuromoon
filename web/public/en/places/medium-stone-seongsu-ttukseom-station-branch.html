Rooftop cafe near Ttukseom Station, renovated from a house.

Medium Stone Seongsu Ttukseom Station is an art/theme cafe renovated from a house, offering a cozy and special atmosphere in the city center. Visitors can enjoy a variety of spaces from the first floor to the rooftop, where they can experience open views and the charm of Seoul Forest. The signature items here are the handmade cakes and various desserts, freshly made daily with quality ingredients, perfectly complementing their coffee. With its stylish interior and comfortable ambiance, it's ideal for dates with friends or partners, solo relaxation, and also suitable for group visits. Conveniently located just 2-3 minutes walk from Ttukseom Station, the cafe also offers group seating, takeout, delivery, and reservations.
