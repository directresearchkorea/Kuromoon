CJ CheilJedang's tenth popup 'Belong' project

CJ CheilJedang's tenth popup 'Belong' is a concept dining project aimed at discovering and nurturing chefs. It operated for about six months from October 7, 2023, in Gangnam-gu, featuring dishes with horse mackerel, mushrooms, monkfish, and duck. Collaborations with Michelin 3-star chefs from Denmark and the UK were also mentioned.
