Euljiro art cafe offering LP music and Italian aperitivo experience.

Jam Aperitivo is a unique art/theme cafe in Euljiro, harmoniously blending LP music with the Italian aperitivo culture. Visitors can immerse themselves in carefully curated LP music, drawing artistic inspiration and enjoying a distinctive visual experience within its stylish ambiance. A signature experience at Jam Aperitivo involves savoring Italian-style aperitivo dishes, perfect for a light pre-dinner treat, all while enjoying the rich sounds of vinyl. Its sophisticated yet comfortable interior makes it an ideal spot for gatherings with friends, romantic dates, or moments of quiet contemplation alone.
