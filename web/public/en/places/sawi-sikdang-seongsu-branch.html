A restaurant in Seongsu where you can enjoy Busan-style Nakgopsae.

Sawi Sikdang Seongsu Branch is a special place in Seongsu-dong, Seoul, where you can enjoy Busan's delicacy, Nakgopsae, with its authentic local flavor. Here, visitors can savor a hearty and spicy Nakgopsae dish, harmoniously blending fresh octopus, chewy tripe, and plump shrimp for a delightful culinary experience. It is highly recommended to order the signature Nakgopsae, which perfectly captures the taste of Busan. This spot is ideal for Seongsu office workers looking for a satisfying lunch or for anyone missing the flavors of Busan cuisine.
