A ring workshop in Seongsu-dong for special memories.

Roventa Seongsu is a unique ring workshop where visitors can create one-of-a-kind rings with loved ones, offering a special memory-making experience. Guests can choose from various ring designs by browsing samples and then craft their own silver rings through a one-day class with expert guidance. A standout feature is the complimentary lifetime cleaning service, ensuring your silver ring remains beautiful without concerns about tarnishing. This romantic and distinctive venue is perfect for couples and friends looking for a unique date spot in Seongsu-dong or a meaningful anniversary gift. While the price point might be slightly higher than other workshops, it reflects the valuable experience and quality service provided.
