Custom cleansing foam making beauty experience popup at Whipped House Seongsu.

Whipped House Seongsu is a beauty experience popup store where visitors can receive a skin diagnosis and create their own customized cleansing foam. Here, you can undergo a professional skin diagnosis to select ingredients optimized for your skin type and enjoy the unique experience of making a one-of-a-kind cleansing foam through a DIY process. The signature activity is the skin diagnosis and pack cleanser making experience, priced at ₩30,000 per person, offering a chance to own a personalized beauty item. This unique experience is highly recommended for friends or couples interested in beauty, and it also provides a satisfying time for individuals looking to create their own special beauty routine. It is advisable to check the operating hours and availability of the experience via the reservation page before your visit.
