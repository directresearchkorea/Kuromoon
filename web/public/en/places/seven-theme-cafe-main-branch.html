An indoor animal experience cafe to meet various animals in Gwangalli.

Seven Theme Cafe Main Branch, located near Gwangalli Beach, is a unique indoor animal experience cafe where visitors can directly touch and feed various animals including mini pigs, parrots, ducks, snakes, and lizards. Guests can create special memories by interacting with cute animals and enjoy the pleasure of observing and experiencing various animal species up close. Although specific signature menus or photo zones are not mentioned in the text, every moment spent with the animals can become a special photo opportunity. With a cozy atmosphere for comfortable interaction with animals indoors, it is highly recommended as a unique date spot for friends and couples, or a family outing destination with children. Parking is available for 1 hour free at Gwangalli Shin-ho Parking Lot or Lotte Parking Lot, and local currency can also be used for convenient visits.
