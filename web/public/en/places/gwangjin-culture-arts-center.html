Gwangjin Culture & Arts Center offers a swimming pool and hosts donation campaigns.

Gwangjin Culture & Arts Center is a multi-cultural complex offering various activities and facilities for local residents. Visitors can enjoy the swimming pool at an affordable price or participate in community engagement activities like the 'BEAUTIFUL Gwangjin Donation Campaign'. The swimming pool operates in multiple time slots, with reasonable fees of ₩3,500 for adults, ₩2,500 for youth, and ₩2,000 for children. This venue is ideal for families or individuals seeking healthy leisure activities, and it also provides opportunities for those wishing to contribute to the local community. The donation campaign runs from August 10 to August 20, 2026, at the Gwangjin Culture & Arts Center's first floor and other locations, so interested participants should visit within this period.
