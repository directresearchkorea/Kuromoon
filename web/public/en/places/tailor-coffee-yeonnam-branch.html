A Blue Ribbon certified coffee shop in Yeonnam-dong.

Tailor Coffee Yeonnam, a prominent coffee spot in Yeonnam-dong, is widely loved for its exceptional coffee quality, proudly certified by the Blue Ribbon sticker. Here, visitors can enjoy a relaxed atmosphere while savoring high-quality coffee and desserts, making for a pleasant and unhurried experience. The signature 'Blue Zi' coffee and the sweet 'Pumpkin Pie' are particularly highlighted as must-try combinations for all guests. Its clean and modern interior provides an ideal setting for dates with friends or partners, as well as for enjoying some peaceful solo time.
