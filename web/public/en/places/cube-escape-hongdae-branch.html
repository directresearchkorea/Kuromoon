A unique themed escape room cafe in Hongdae.

Cube Escape Hongdae Branch is a unique themed concept dining and escape room cafe located in Hongdae. Visitors can enjoy thrilling deduction games in immersive spaces with diverse stories like 'Secret of the Pyramid' and 'Hansel and Gretel,' combined with a special culinary experience. Each theme offers unique backdrops and devices, allowing guests to savor signature menus or drinks that complement the game, providing a special experience as if they're characters in a movie. This venue is highly recommended for friends and couples seeking a distinctive indoor date or for those looking for team-building activities paired with a unique meal. It is advisable to secure your time in advance through Naver reservation before visiting, and for same-day visits, checking availability via their website is recommended.
