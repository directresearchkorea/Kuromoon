Unique one-day class near Gyeongbokgung for gemstone bracelet making and chakra therapy.

Stone Bless You is a unique workshop located near Gyeongbokgung, offering visitors healing and distinctive experiences through gemstone bracelet making and chakra & gemstone therapy classes. Here, you can choose and combine various gemstones to create your own one-of-a-kind bracelet, and enjoy a sensory time finding inner balance through chakra therapy. The chakra & gemstone therapy class is a signature experience unique to Stone Bless You, providing deep healing using gemstones tailored to individual energy states. The cozy and peaceful atmosphere of the space is perfect for couples seeking a unique date course, and also great for creating special memories with friends or enjoying a quiet healing time alone.
