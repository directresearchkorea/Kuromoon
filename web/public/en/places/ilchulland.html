Jeju theme cafe offering media art and foot bath experiences.

Ilchulland is a unique theme cafe in Seogwipo, Jeju, where visitors can simultaneously enjoy media art and a relaxing foot bath experience. Guests can immerse themselves in visual delight by appreciating beautiful media art exhibitions and rejuvenate their body and mind with a distinctive foot bath experience. The signature offering here is undoubtedly the foot bath combined with media art, complemented by a variety of beverages and desserts. This artistic and comfortable space is perfect for friends, couples, and families traveling to eastern Jeju, offering a special memory.
