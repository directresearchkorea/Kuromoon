Affordable art workshop experience in Suwon Haenggung-dong for ₩22,000.

Located in Suwon Haenggung-dong, 'Nakseo Jigeum' is a unique and cost-effective art workshop where visitors can enjoy various art experiences for a reasonable price of ₩22,000. Guests can create their own unique artworks through diverse painting activities such as pattern painting, backdrop painting, and mirror painting, with large Bearbrick painting being particularly popular for special gifts. This workshop offers a quiet and cozy atmosphere, making it a perfect indoor spot for couples on a date, friends seeking a special time, or anyone wanting to create meaningful handmade gifts for family. Previous visitors have highly praised its excellent value, recommending it for special occasions like a father's birthday gift.
