A fair-trade chocolate concept cafe in Gujwa-eup, Jeju.

Cocoha Cacao Family is a concept cafe located in Gujwa-eup, Jeju-si, Jeju-do, which goes beyond a simple dessert shop by directly making chocolates and pursuing a sustainable agricultural ecosystem through fair trade. It's particularly good for experiencing fresh and ethically produced cacao products in a rustic yet friendly atmosphere, driven by the owner's admirable motto. It is highly recommended for those who wish to enjoy various cacao products like chocolates and nut butters, while also engaging in consumption that supports sustainable values. This cafe is famous for its homemade chocolates and nut butters, and visitors can also enjoy espresso bar menus, offering a unique culinary experience.
