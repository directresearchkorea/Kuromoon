Professional hair salon located in Yongsan I'Park Mall.

Junohair Yongsan I'Park Mall Branch is a premium hair salon conveniently located within I'Park Mall, directly connected to Yongsan Station. Visitors can experience a variety of trendy hair treatments, including men's down perms, layered cuts, and layered perms, with a special focus on revitalizing head spa services for scalp and hair health. Signature services include the precise down perm for men and stylish layered cuts and perms for women, all crafted by skilled designers. The salon boasts a modern and sophisticated interior, offering a comfortable and pleasant atmosphere perfect for friends, couples, or even a solo pampering session. Its prime location ensures easy access via Yongsan Station, and ample parking is available within I'Park Mall for those arriving by car.
