PNB Pungnyeon Bakery Main Store, famous for its Choco Pie in Jeonju.

PNB Pungnyeon Bakery Main Store, representing Jeonju, is a bakery boasting a long history and tradition, gaining nationwide fame especially for its signature Choco Pie. Visitors can enjoy freshly baked bread and various desserts, making it a must-visit spot for Jeonju travelers and popular for souvenir purchases. The undeniable signature menu item here is the PNB Choco Pie, beloved by all ages for its sweet and soft taste, which can be enjoyed after natural thawing from frozen storage. Located near Jeonju Hanok Village, it is an ideal place for travelers to stop by for a snack or to buy gifts for family and friends.
