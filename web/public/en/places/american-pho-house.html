American Pho House, located in Seoul's Manri-dong and Euljiro, is an American-style pho specialty restaurant renowned for its deep and rich broth. Visitors can enjoy a clean yet satisfying meal with pho generously topped with meat. The "meat topping bomb" pho is a signature dish, earning praise as a life-changing pho experience from many visitors. With a casual and comfortable atmosphere, it's perfect for dates with friends or partners, or for a hearty solo meal.
