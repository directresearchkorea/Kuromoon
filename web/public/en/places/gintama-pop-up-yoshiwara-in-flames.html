Gintama Yoshiwara in Flames pop-up store in Suwon

This is a special pop-up store themed after the popular anime Gintama's 'Yoshiwara in Flames' arc. Visitors can explore a wide range of Gintama merchandise, enjoying a more relaxed shopping experience at the Suwon AK Mall location compared to the crowded Hongdae branch. A variety of Gintama goods are available, offering fans the joy of discovering collectible items. It's an ideal spot for anime lovers to visit with friends or partners, creating special memories together. While the first week operated by pre-reservation, subsequent visits are possible without a reservation, and the Suwon AK Mall branch offers the advantage of entry without long queues.
