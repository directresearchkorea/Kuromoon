A spacious, comfortable large-scale botanical garden themed cafe in Geoje.

Eomma's Sea Park Cafe in Geoje, Gyeongsangnam-do, is a large botanical garden themed cafe featuring spacious, comfortable indoor areas and artistic interiors. Visitors can enjoy a leisurely time amidst lush plants and harmonious artworks, experiencing a unique atmosphere where nature and art blend seamlessly. Beyond a variety of beverages and desserts, numerous photo zones set against the backdrop of plants and art pieces allow guests to capture beautiful memories. Its nature-friendly yet sophisticated interior offers a comfortable and special retreat for friends, couples, and even families. Equipped with a large parking lot and an elevator, it provides convenient access, making it a relatively relaxed destination even on weekends.
