Chef Kim Sung-woon's seasonal ingredient dining in Hannam-dong.

Table for Four, a fine dining establishment by Chef Kim Sung-woon, offers an exquisite culinary journey with seasonal ingredients directly sourced from Taean. Guests can enjoy the chef's creative lunch or dinner courses, inspired by "Black and White Chef," while taking in dazzling views from a window seat. The chef's signature dishes are an absolute must-try for an unforgettable experience. Its luxurious and romantic ambiance makes it perfect for special anniversary meals or dates with loved ones, and reservations are essential for occasions like birthdays. Booking a window seat in advance is highly recommended due to its popularity.
