A beautiful garden vegan bakery cafe located in Sunchang.

Berjardang, located in Sunchang, Jeollabuk-do, is an emotional cafe renowned for its beautiful garden and vegan bakery. This place offers visitors a visual delight with its expansive green landscape and spaces that capture the colors of each season, making it a popular spot for photography. It's an ideal destination for those looking to enjoy delicious bread while admiring the wonderful surrounding scenery and spending leisurely time, highly recommended for anyone wishing to create special memories away from daily life. The bread and the stunning garden views are particularly known as signature experiences here.
