NC Department Store Gangseo Branch, a multi-purpose venue for dining and shopping.

NC Department Store Gangseo Branch, part of the E-Land Group, is a comprehensive cultural space offering diverse shopping and dining experiences under one roof. Visitors can enjoy meals at popular restaurants like Sam Baek Jip Kongnamul Gukbap or explore the latest women's fashion trends at stores such as The Izzat Live. Sam Baek Jip's refreshing bean sprout soup is a must-try signature dish, while various fashion items help visitors complete their unique style. This vibrant department store is ideal for friends and families seeking a weekend outing, or for individuals planning a relaxed shopping and dining experience. Convenient parking facilities are available for those driving, and popular dining spots might have a short wait during peak meal times.
