Hanwoo omakase restaurant in Majang-dong, ₩110,000 per person course.

Hanwoo Dream Main Branch is a premium Hanwoo omakase restaurant in Majang-dong, offering a luxurious Hanwoo party experience even on ordinary days. Visitors can savor various cuts of the finest Hanwoo prepared daily, alongside a unique Hanwoo curry that tastes like it's been simmered for 100 hours. The ₩110,000 per person course features a selection of Hanwoo dishes including brisket and flap meat, with the rich Hanwoo curry being a must-try. With its upscale yet comfortable ambiance, it's highly recommended for friends or couples seeking a special dining experience with top-tier Korean beef. Note that it's a bit of a walk from Wangsimni Station, so consider transportation options when planning your visit.
