Hippocampus Seongsu

🔥 Over 622 Naver blog reviews
✅ Recent status: 운영 중

A perfume workshop in Seongsu, perfect for dates, located 5 minutes from Seongsu Station.

'Hippocampus Seongsu' is a perfume workshop nestled in the vibrant Seongsu district, offering visitors a unique opportunity to craft their own one-of-a-kind fragrance. Here, guests can indulge in a sensory journey by combining various aromatic ingredients, enjoying the creative pleasure of designing a personalized perfume. The distinctive perfume created by hand becomes more than just a product; it transforms into a special signature item imbued with the visitor's memories and emotions. Providing a cozy and emotional atmosphere within the trendy Seongsu area, it's an ideal spot for couples seeking a unique date experience or friends looking to create special memories together. Conveniently located just a 5-minute walk from Seongsu Station, it boasts excellent accessibility, but as there is no dedicated parking, public transportation is highly recommended for a smooth visit.
