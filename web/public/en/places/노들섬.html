King Jeongjo's Royal Parade reenactment event at Nodeulseom.

The King Jeongjo's Royal Parade Reenactment at Nodeulseom is an event that moves for about 2 hours from Sookmyung Women's University Station, through Yongsan Station, to Nodeulseom. At Nodeulseom and Nodeul Naru Park, visitors can enjoy various attractions, including an 'Assassin Battle' reenactment, along with commentary from history lecturer Choi Taesung.
