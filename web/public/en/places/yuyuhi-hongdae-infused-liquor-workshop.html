Infused liquor one-day class workshop near Hongdae Station.

Located near Hongdae Station, 'Yuyuhi Hongdae Infused Liquor Workshop' offers a unique one-day class where visitors can craft their own special infused liquor. Participants can experience the process of making personalized infused liquor using various ingredients and take home their handmade creation. Here, you can learn diverse infused liquor recipes made with fresh ingredients and enjoy the pleasure of completing your own unique drink. This indoor activity is highly recommended for creating special memories with friends, enjoying a unique date with a partner, or pursuing a hobby for solo time. With excellent accessibility just a 1-minute walk from Hongdae Station, it's convenient for public transport, and it's known as a place visited by celebrities like TripleS Nakyung.
