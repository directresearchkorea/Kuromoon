Experience Munich's Oktoberfest in Seoul

Oktoberfest Seoul 2026 is the first event in Korea officially approved by the city of Munich to use its name. Held at Oil Tank Culture Park in Mapo-gu, Seoul, it offers various Bavarian Festbiers, including Weltenburger, the world's oldest monastery beer.
