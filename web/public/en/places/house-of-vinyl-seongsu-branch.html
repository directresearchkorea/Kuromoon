A quiet LP cafe in Seongsu, perfect for music appreciation and dates.

House of Vinyl Seongsu is an art/theme cafe in Seongsu-dong where visitors can enjoy a special time appreciating LP music. Guests can experience a comfortable and emotional atmosphere by listening to various vinyl records in a quiet and spacious setting. The main charm of this place is the unique cultural experience of selecting and listening to LPs, offering visitors unforgettable memories. This cafe with its pleasant ambiance is highly recommended for couples seeking a unique date spot or individuals looking for a peaceful time alone.
