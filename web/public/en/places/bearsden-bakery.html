A popular bakery cafe in Yeonnam-dong known for its beautiful exterior and delicious bread.

Bearsden Bakery is a delightful dessert spot located in Yeonnam-dong, captivating visitors with its beautiful exterior and an array of visually appealing breads and pastries. Guests can enjoy a relaxing time across its spacious first and second floors, savoring a wide variety of delicious baked goods and desserts. The chestnut bread, often described as a work of art, and the crispy, flavorful croissant sandwiches are highly recommended as must-try signature items. With its charming interior and delectable treats, it offers a perfect ambiance for dates with friends or loved ones, or for enjoying a sweet moment alone.
