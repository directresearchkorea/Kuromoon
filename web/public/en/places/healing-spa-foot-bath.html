A healing space in Jeju offering a special foot bath spa experience with your mom.

Jeju Healing Spa Foot Bath is a special wellness space designed to heal the tired body and mind through warm foot baths and emotional spa experiences. Visitors can immerse their feet in warm water, feeling comfort and enjoying a sensory experience of complete relaxation in a quiet atmosphere. The Foot Bath Healing course is representative, where starting with a warm foot bath to relax the body is the core experience here. This place is recommended for those who wish to take a quiet break from their busy daily lives, especially those looking to spend special time with their parents.
