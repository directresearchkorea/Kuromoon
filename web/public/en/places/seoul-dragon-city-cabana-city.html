Hotel concept dining with a Han River view rooftop experience.

Seoul Dragon City Cabana City is a concept dining space where visitors can enjoy an exotic resort atmosphere on a rooftop with a Han River view. Located on the 34th floor of Sky Kingdom, it offers panoramic views, opportunities to enjoy pool parties, and benefits like a welcome drink and an overnight stay through the 'Midnight Bites' package. The hotel pool party is a key highlight, providing guests with a unique experience. The exotic interior, designed to transport guests to a resort the moment they step out of the elevator, is recommended for special dates with loved ones or fun gatherings with friends. It is directly accessible from Yongsan Station via a connecting passage, and it's advisable to plan ahead as it can be crowded on weekends or pool party days.
