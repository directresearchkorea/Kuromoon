Art/theme brunch cafe located in Yangjaecheon.

Nestled in Yangjaecheon, Little Lunch Club is a unique brunch cafe where art and theme harmoniously blend. Visitors can enjoy fresh brunch menus, including sandwiches made with chewy fresh bread, and the pet-friendly atmosphere allows for a more special experience. The chewy fresh bread sandwich, in particular, is a signature menu item here and a must-try delicacy. Its cozy and comfortable interior makes it perfect for spending a relaxed weekend with friends, loved ones, or pets, or even for enjoying some solo time. As parking space is limited, it is convenient to use nearby public parking lots, and on weekdays, you can visit without waiting.
