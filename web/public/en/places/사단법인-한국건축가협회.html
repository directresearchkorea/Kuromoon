A festival exploring urban architecture and eco-friendly materials

The Seoul Architecture Festival explores various aspects of urban architecture. It introduces modern architectural materials, such as eco-friendly building exteriors, and highlights the present and future of architecture through projects by renowned architects from Korea and abroad. It takes place at the Korean Institute of Architects within the Daehanminguk Yesulin Center.
