Double Lovers Jeju Flagship Store

Fashion sunglasses flagship store located on Aewol Cafe Street in Jeju.

Double Lovers Jeju Flagship Store, nestled on Aewol Cafe Street, is a fashion sunglasses specialty shop offering a unique retail experience. Visitors are greeted by a striking giant cat sculpture at the entrance, which doubles as a popular photo zone, inviting them to explore and try on a diverse range of stylish sunglasses. Here, you can discover Double Lovers' distinctive and trendy eyewear, perfect for adding a fashionable touch to your look or as a special souvenir from Jeju. The store's chic and contemporary interior makes it an excellent destination for friends or couples seeking a fun shopping excursion, allowing them to immerse themselves in the vibrant atmosphere of Aewol Cafe Street. For convenience, parking is available at nearby public or paid parking facilities.
