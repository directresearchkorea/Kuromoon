Hwajeonri Bakery's Cloud Bread pop-up at The Hyundai Daegu.

Hwajeonri Bakery, a renowned bakery cafe from Gijang, Busan, is hosting a 'Cloud Bread' pop-up store in the B1 pop-up zone of The Hyundai Daegu. This special event offers a unique opportunity to taste their famous fluffy Cloud Bread, known for its soft texture, right here in Daegu. The soft and moist Cloud Bread is a must-try signature item that attracts bread enthusiasts on a pilgrimage. It's a great spot to enjoy delicious bread with friends or loved ones amidst the lively atmosphere of the department store's food hall, highly recommended for all bread lovers. The pop-up runs from August 7th to 13th, and it's advisable to avoid visiting on August 10th (Monday), which is a department store holiday.
