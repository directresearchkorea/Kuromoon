A tranquil brunch cafe in Aewol, Jeju.

Aewol Brunch Cafe The Bricks 247 is a tranquil and charming brunch cafe located in a beautiful village in Aewol, Jeju. Visitors can enjoy a relaxing time appreciating its attractive exterior and cozy interior, and also indulge in various cafe menus in addition to brunch. Here, you can savor brunch menus made with fresh ingredients, and the beautiful space itself serves as a signature photo zone. Its calm and cozy atmosphere is perfect for dates with friends or couples, as well as for spending peaceful solo time. The cafe is closed every Sunday and operates from 10:30 AM to 5:00 PM from Monday to Saturday, so it's advisable to check the operating hours before visiting.
