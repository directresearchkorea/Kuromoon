Affordable sushi omakase restaurant near Ttukseom Station in Seongsu.

Sushi Oomoto, located near Ttukseom Station in Seongsu, is an affordable sushi omakase restaurant perfect for celebrating special occasions. Visitors can enjoy a refined dining experience with fresh sushi courses paired with various alcoholic beverages, including Spanish Albariño wine. The unique presentation of appetizers served all at once and the chef's meticulously prepared sushi course are considered signature offerings here. It's an ideal spot for couples or friends celebrating birthdays and anniversaries, offering a satisfying culinary journey in a cozy and neat ambiance. Note that there is a break time from 3 PM to 6 PM, so checking the operating hours before visiting is recommended for a smooth experience.
