A wellness space offering beauty treatments and health products.

Wellness House Seoul is a comprehensive wellness space where visitors can experience beauty items that aid post-dermatological treatment care and various healthy wellness products. Guests can directly try out beauty products suitable for use after procedures like Potenza Juvelook and enjoy healthy wellness smoothies from Twelve Wonder Bar to nurture their mind and body. Signature wellness products such as Swellnishi Pith and Koper, along with the nutritious smoothies, are must-try items. This sophisticated space is perfect for modern individuals seeking both health and beauty, recommended for friends, couples, or those desiring a personal healing time focused on skincare. Convenient valet parking service is available for easy access.
