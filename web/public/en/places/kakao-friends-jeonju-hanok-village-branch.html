Cute Kakao Friends merchandise shop in Jeonju Hanok Village.

Kakao Friends Jeonju Hanok Village Branch is a special character merchandise store nestled in Jeonju Hanok Village, where Korea's traditional beauty thrives. Visitors can enjoy exploring a variety of new Kakao Friends merchandise featuring characters like Ryan and Apeach, along with exclusive items unique to the Hanok Village branch. The adorable character goods particularly capture visitors' attention, making them popular as special souvenirs or gifts from a trip to Jeonju. With its charming and lively atmosphere, it's a great place for friends or couples to create memories, or for individuals to browse cute items during their solo time. Conveniently located within the main tourist route of Jeonju Hanok Village, it offers easy access for visitors to freely shop without prior reservations.
