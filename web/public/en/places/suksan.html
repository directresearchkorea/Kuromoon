Large bakery cafe in Mokpo with views of Yudalsan Mountain and the sea.

SUKSAN is a large bakery cafe in Mokpo offering simultaneous views of Yudalsan Mountain and the beautiful sea. Visitors can enjoy a relaxing time while admiring the expansive scenery, savoring a variety of fresh, delicious breads and aromatic coffee. Even those who typically don't prefer bread are satisfied, making the bakery items a must-try signature of this cafe. With its spacious and pleasant interior and excellent views, it's an ideal spot for creating special memories with friends or loved ones.
