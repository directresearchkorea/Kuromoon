Kobe Gyukatsu Seoul Gangnam Branch

🔥 Over 6497 Naver blog reviews
✅ Recent status: 운영 중

A Gangnam Station restaurant where you can enjoy melt-in-your-mouth gyukatsu.

Located just 5 minutes from Gangnam Station Exit 11, Kobe Gyukatsu Seoul Gangnam Branch is a dining spot specializing in traditional gyukatsu. The gyukatsu here features a thin, crispy coating encasing tender, pink beef that melts in your mouth, offering an exquisite taste. Beyond gyukatsu, you can also enjoy various other dishes like udon or spicy buldak-myeon, making it highly recommended for those seeking a diverse culinary experience. The unique combination of melt-in-your-mouth gyukatsu and spicy buldak-myeon is particularly highlighted as a special charm of this place.
