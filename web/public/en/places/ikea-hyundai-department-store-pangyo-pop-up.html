IKEA pop-up store at Hyundai Department Store Pangyo

The IKEA Hyundai Department Store Pangyo pop-up offers a unique opportunity to conveniently experience various products from the Swedish lifestyle brand IKEA within a department store setting. Visitors can enjoy browsing charming design items like the Bichehyang Miffy Edition, and also explore fresh produce in nearby food sections. The Bichehyang Miffy Edition, specifically noted as desirable in reviews, stands out as a must-see signature item. Located within the pleasant environment of Hyundai Department Store, this pop-up is perfect for a casual visit while shopping, highly recommended for friends, couples, or families interested in IKEA products. The pop-up runs until August 17th, so it's advisable to check the dates before visiting.
