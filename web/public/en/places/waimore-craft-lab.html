Craft beer brewing one-day class in Jamsil, Seoul.

Waimore Craft Lab in Jamsil, Seoul, offers a unique experience where visitors can brew their own craft beer. Guests can learn the beer-making process through a one-day class and savor various craft beers brewed in-house. The 'Bbangding WEST COAST IPA,' known for its rich hop aroma and bitter depth, is a must-try signature brew. This atmospheric venue is perfect for dates with friends or partners, or even company team-building events, promising an unforgettable memory for beer lovers.
