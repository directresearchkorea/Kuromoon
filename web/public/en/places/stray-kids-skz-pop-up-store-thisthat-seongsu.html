Stray Kids SKZ Pop-up Store This&That (Seongsu)

🔥 Over 5 Naver blog reviews
✅ Recent status: 운영 중

A pop-up store in Seongsu for Stray Kids fans.

This pop-up store is themed around the popular K-pop group Stray Kids' 'This&That,' offering a special experience for fans. Visitors can immerse themselves in the Stray Kids universe, enjoying various merchandise and exhibition spaces that create a sense of connection with the members. Although some reviews mentioned a lack of items to purchase, fans can still create cherished memories with dedicated photo zones and commemorative items. It is highly recommended for friends, couples, or solo fans who love K-POP idols and wish to experience the vibrant fandom atmosphere firsthand. The pop-up store appeared to operate on a weekly basis, so it is advisable to check the exact operating period and reservation details before visiting.
