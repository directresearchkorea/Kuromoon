Marie Laurencin retrospective exhibition at My Art Museum

The Marie Laurencin retrospective exhibition at My Art Museum is conveniently located just a 2-minute walk from Samseong Station Exit 4. Visitors can immerse themselves in Marie Laurencin's unique artistic world, which particularly resonates with women's sensibilities and is highly regarded as appealing to Korean audiences. Through her works, which reflect a lonely and melancholic life, the exhibition offers a profound emotional and contemplative experience, providing a special opportunity to connect with the forgotten artist's sentiments. This exhibition goes beyond mere viewing, offering a rich cultural experience that allows one to delve into and empathize with the artist's inner world.
