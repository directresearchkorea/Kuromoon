Korean restaurant near Busan Cultural Center, offering bulgogi ssam-bap with various fresh greens.

Munhwa Ssambap, nestled near the Busan Cultural Center, offers a unique dining concept where guests can savor generous Korean ssam-bap accompanied by an abundance of fresh leafy greens. Visitors can enjoy a wholesome and fulfilling meal by wrapping the signature bulgogi ssam-bap with various types of crisp vegetables. This must-try dish, perfectly complemented by an array of diverse side dishes, delivers a rich and satisfying flavor experience. With its comfortable and tidy ambiance, it's highly recommended for families and friends seeking a healthy Korean meal, making it an excellent choice for anyone exploring Daeyeon-dong, Busan.
