Mippudong Seongsu

🔥 Over 5581 Naver blog reviews
✅ Recent status: 운영 중

A Seongsu restaurant offering fresh seafood rice bowls.

Mippudong Seongsu is a specialized seafood rice bowl restaurant located in Seongsu-dong, offering a variety of dishes made with fresh ingredients. Their signature Mippudong, featuring nine rich ingredients like tuna, salmon, and uni, along with the seasonal tomato salmon noodles, are particularly impressive. It is highly recommended for those who enjoy fresh seafood or seek a unique Japanese dining experience, with convenient Naver reservations available. Public parking at Ttukseom Yusu-ji is accessible, making it convenient for visitors arriving by car.
