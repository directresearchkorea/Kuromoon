Fusion Korean dining in a traditional hanok setting in Ikseon-dong.

Jang & Paste Ikseon is a fusion Korean dining restaurant that harmonizes with the tranquil hanok atmosphere of Ikseon-dong. The tables are spaciously arranged, allowing visitors to escape the noisy city and enjoy comfortable conversations over their meal. It is highly recommended for couples planning a special date, offering delicious fusion Korean cuisine amidst the beautiful traditional hanok charm of Ikseon-dong. The overall calm and cozy interior provides a relaxed and satisfying dining experience for guests.
