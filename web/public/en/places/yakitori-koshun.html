Premium yakitori dining specializing in charcoal-grilled skewers and seasonal dishes.

Yakitori Koshun in Apgujeong is a concept dining spot offering premium yakitori grilled over high-quality charcoal, alongside seasonal dishes. Guests can vividly enjoy the chef's delicate live cooking process from the bar counter, experiencing a variety of yakitori menus paired with diverse alcoholic beverages like sake and beer. The harmony between the food and drinks is exceptional, even captivating those who typically prefer sweeter alcoholic beverages to the charm of sake. This establishment is highly recommended for couples or friends seeking a special gourmet experience in a sophisticated atmosphere, where sake pairing can further enrich their meal.
