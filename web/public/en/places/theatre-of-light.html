Immersive media art exhibition at Walkerhill Hotel

The Theatre of Light is an immersive media art exhibition space located on the B1 floor of Walkerhill Hotel in Gwangjin-gu, Seoul. It offers a special cultural experience to both hotel guests and general visitors, providing visual and auditory pleasure through various works utilizing light and sound. It is particularly recommended for those looking to enjoy a relaxing time in conjunction with a staycation, and it is convenient to use alongside other hotel facilities. We highly recommend it to anyone wishing to create special memories while appreciating diverse media art exhibitions, and visitors can also enjoy the lingering impression of the viewing while relaxing at the cafe within the exhibition hall.
