Gentle Monster Haus Nowhere Dosan's Prada collaboration pop-up.

Gentle Monster Haus Nowhere Dosan is a unique pop-up space showcasing a special collaboration between fashion brand Gentle Monster and Prada. Visitors can be among the first to explore new eyewear and sunglasses collections, even enduring long queues on release days. The Prada collaboration items, in particular, attract significant attention, prompting early queues, and visitors can also enjoy Nudake's distinctive desserts. This sensory space, where fashion and art converge, is ideal for trend-sensitive friends or couples looking to share a unique experience. On popular product release days, large crowds gather before opening, so early arrival or anticipating a wait is essential for a more relaxed visit.
