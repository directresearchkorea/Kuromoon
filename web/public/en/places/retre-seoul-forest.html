Retre Seoul Forest

🔥 Over 700 Naver blog reviews
✅ Recent status: 운영 중

Create your unique perfume through brain measurement at this Seongsu workshop.

Retre Seoul Forest is a unique perfume workshop where you can create your one-of-a-kind fragrance through brain measurement. Visitors can enjoy a special sensory experience, discovering the scent that best suits them with the help of a delicate perfumer. Here, you can not only make your own basic perfume but also craft a diffuser, resulting in a more meaningful keepsake. With multiple branches in the Seongsu area, it's easily accessible and highly recommended for couples or friends looking to create special memories together. It's advisable to check the operating hours (daily 12:00 - 20:30) and prices (perfume ₩35,000, diffuser ₩45,000) before your visit.
