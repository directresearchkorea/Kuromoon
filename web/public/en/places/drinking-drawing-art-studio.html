Unique one-day drawing classes at an art studio in Jamsil.

Drinking Drawing Art Studio in Jamsil offers unique one-day drawing classes, providing a special space to create art and enjoy a memorable time. Visitors can immerse themselves in various classes like acrylic painting, Bearbrick art, neon drawing, and pen drawing, experiencing the joy of completing their own masterpieces. The acrylic painting class is particularly popular for couples, allowing participants to learn diverse drawing techniques. With a relaxed and creative atmosphere, it's an ideal spot for friends, couples, or individuals to make special memories or enjoy artistic solitude. The studio is regularly closed on Tuesdays, and it's advisable to check the operating hours (13:00-21:00, with breaks from 15:00-16:00 and 18:00-19:00) before visiting.
