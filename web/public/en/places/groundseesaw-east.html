Media art exhibition space by artist Lee Ki-hoon

Groundseesaw East is a cultural complex offering profound contemplation and novel experiences through media art exhibitions, notably featuring two contrasting shows by artist Lee Ki-hoon: 'Garden of Today' and 'Paradise of Tomorrow'. 'Garden of Today' provides a serene escape from daily life, while 'Paradise of Tomorrow' offers an intense, immersive experience that provokes thought and doubt about the future. Lee Ki-hoon's original exhibition 'Paradise of Tomorrow' is a must-see highlight, and each exhibition area naturally serves as a unique photo zone for capturing special memories. The modern and sensory interior is ideal for those seeking artistic inspiration, recommended for friends, couples on a unique date, or individuals desiring deep artistic reflection. Reservations can be conveniently made via Naver Booking, and for popular exhibitions, it is advisable to book in advance to ensure a relaxed viewing experience.
