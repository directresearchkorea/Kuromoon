Kakao Friends character shop on the 8th floor of Shinsegae Department Store Dongdaegu.

This Kakao Friends character shop, located on the 8th floor of Shinsegae Department Store Dongdaegu, offers a delightful space to meet beloved Kakao Friends characters. Visitors can browse and purchase a wide array of Kakao Friends merchandise, enjoying a pleasant indoor experience perfect for a comfortable date. The store features popular characters like Chun-sik, with its eye-catching exterior serving as an inviting photo zone. With its bright and lively atmosphere, it's highly recommended for friends and couples looking to shop for character goods or enjoy a cool indoor activity, especially during hot weather. Conveniently situated within Shinsegae Department Store, connected to Dongdaegu Station, it operates daily from 10:20 to 21:30, except on department store holidays.
