Unique rooftop cafe in Gangneung, famous for its pepper coffee.

Cafe Ijinri in Gangneung is a unique concept cafe that has transformed an old house into a cozy and distinctive space. It is particularly famous for its signature 'pepper coffee,' making it one of Gangneung's representative attractions alongside Toemaru Cafe. This cafe is highly recommended for visitors seeking a special coffee experience in a tranquil setting, where they can also enjoy the interesting backstory that its name 'Ijinri' originated from 'Easily.' Its proximity to the Red Hammer prop shop makes it a convenient stop for a combined visit.
