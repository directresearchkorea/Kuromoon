A Harry Potter themed cafe recreating a fantasy world from the movies.

Hongdae 943 King's Cross is a unique Harry Potter themed cafe that transforms the entire building into a fantasy world from the movies. Visitors can enjoy a magical visual experience, feeling as if they've stepped into the film, from the distinctive brick exterior to the enchanting interior atmosphere. Here, you can savor unique menu items that enhance the movie's ambiance, along with wizard kits, making it perfect for creating special memories. This space is ideal for friends, couples, or families with children who love fantasy films, offering a truly distinctive experience.
