A large gallery cafe converted from an old textile factory.

Seongsu-dong Daelim Warehouse Gallery is a large gallery cafe transformed from an old textile factory, offering a unique space where retro charm and artistic ambiance coexist. Its high ceilings and massive wooden doors are striking, allowing visitors to enjoy various art pieces in the gallery while spending leisurely time. You can savor a variety of beverages and desserts, including cold brew iced tea and oat matcha, with the space itself serving as an excellent photo zone. The retro yet modern interior is perfect for meeting friends, a date course with a loved one, or even for solitary contemplation.
