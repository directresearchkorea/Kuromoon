A drawing cafe in Gwangju's Chungjang-ro.

Drawing Cafe Hansel, located in Gwangju's Chungjang-ro, is an art/theme cafe offering visitors a unique experience to awaken their artistic senses by drawing. In its charming basement space adorned with pretty yellow borders, guests can create their own masterpieces through drawing experiences, and also enjoy browsing various stickers and masking tapes or getting their drawing books signed. The signature experience here is creating your own drawing book and engaging in the drawing activity itself, providing a special time to unleash creativity. Its cute and pretty interior makes it an ideal spot for families with children to create cherished memories, and it's also recommended for unique dates with friends or partners. Parking is convenient at Boseong Parking Lot, and arriving around 11:20 AM is suggested.
