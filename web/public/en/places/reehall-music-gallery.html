A unique music listening cafe in Seongbuk-dong offering a special LP music experience.

Reehall Music Gallery is a special music listening room that offers an in-depth music appreciation experience beyond a simple LP cafe. Here, visitors can fully enjoy analog-sensibility music through a diverse collection of LP records and high-quality audio systems, providing a unique visual and auditory experience. Rather than specific signature menus, the core content is the LP music listening itself, allowing guests to immerse themselves in music that suits their taste. Nestled deep within Seongbuk-dong, its cozy and tranquil atmosphere makes it an ideal unique date spot for couples or a perfect retreat for music lovers. As it primarily operates only on weekends, it is advisable to check the operating days before visiting.
