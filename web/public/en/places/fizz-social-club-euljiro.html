Hidden art-themed cafe in Euljiro's alley, playing LP music.

Fizz Social Club Euljiro, nestled deep within an Euljiro alley, is a unique art-themed cafe where LP music and artistic sensibilities harmoniously blend. Visitors can enjoy a visual and auditory experience, listening to analog LP music from a turntable, feeling as if they are on a relaxing vacation in the city. Here, a cool cup of coffee accompanied by LP music becomes a special signature experience that soothes a weary mind. The cozy intimacy of its small space and emotional interior create an ideal atmosphere for quiet contemplation or intimate conversations with a loved one. Located on the 4th floor without an elevator, it is advisable to make a reservation via Catchtable if seats are unavailable before visiting.
