Ida, a Korean concept dining restaurant located on Seosunra-gil.

Located on Seosunra-gil, 'Ida' is a concept dining space that reinterprets the beauty of Korea in a modern way. Here, visitors can enjoy a gourmet experience that satisfies all senses through elegant and high-quality Korean cuisine. A variety of seasonal menus are prepared, with dishes that highlight traditional flavors being particularly impressive. The cozy interior, harmonizing with the tranquil charm of Seosunra-gil, is suitable for romantic dates or special gatherings with loved ones. Reservations are recommended prior to visiting for a comfortable and leisurely dining experience.
