A restaurant in Namyangju specializing in hearty handmade dumpling hotpot.

'Cheonmasan Handmade Dumplings', nestled in Namyangju's Cheonmasan area, is a Korean restaurant renowned for its homemade dumplings and generous dumpling hotpot. Visitors can savor a hearty dumpling hotpot featuring large short ribs and plenty of beef brisket, accompanied by a variety of meticulously prepared side dishes. The must-try signature dishes include the dumpling hotpot with its rich and deep broth, chewy steamed dumplings, and the unique meat noodles. With a warm and friendly atmosphere reflecting the owner's culinary skill, it's an excellent spot for families and friends to enjoy a satisfying meal together. Handmade dumplings are also available for takeout, allowing customers to enjoy their delicious taste at home.
