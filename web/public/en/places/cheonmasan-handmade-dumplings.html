Cheonmasan Handmade Dumplings in Namyangju, famous for hot pot and noodle soup.

Cheonmasan Handmade Dumplings is a renowned Korean restaurant in Namyangju, famous for its generous mandu-jeongol (dumpling hot pot) and meticulously prepared handmade dumplings. Visitors can savor the rich flavors of the mandu-jeongol, which comes with large galbi ribs and plenty of beef, or enjoy the delicious gogi-guksu (meat noodles) and steamed mandu, all showcasing the owner's excellent culinary skills. The mandu-jeongol is so hearty it could be mistaken for galbi-tang, and the steamed dumplings are available for takeout, allowing guests to enjoy them at home. With its warm and inviting atmosphere, complemented by homemade side dishes, this spot is perfect for family meals or gatherings with friends seeking an authentic Korean dining experience. Takeout options for dumplings are available.
