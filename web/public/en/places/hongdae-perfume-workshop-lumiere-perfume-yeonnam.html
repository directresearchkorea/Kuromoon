Perfume making one-day class workshop near Hongdae Station.

Lumiere Perfume Yeonnam, located near Hongdae Station, is a unique one-day class workshop where visitors can create their own special perfumes. Guests can directly sample and combine various fragrance notes, crafting a one-of-a-kind signature scent. With the guidance of a professional perfumer, participants select ingredients tailored to their preferences, making the perfume creation process a memorable experience. The cozy and emotional atmosphere is perfect for an unusual date with friends or a partner, or for solo visitors looking to discover a new hobby. Conveniently located an 8-minute walk from Hongdae Entrance Station Exit 3, it's advisable to inquire about reservations in advance for a smoother experience.
