TFT Wild Fan Fest pop-up at The Hyundai Seoul.

The 'TFT Wild Fan Fest' at The Hyundai Seoul is a special pop-up store themed around the popular game TFT. Visitors can enjoy various activities, including the Liftbound experience stamp tour, across the 5th-floor Sounds Forest and the 6th floor. The stamp tour is a core content of this event, offering a unique experience for participants. This space is ideal for game fans to gather and have a great time, highly recommended for friends or couples to visit and experience the game world firsthand.
