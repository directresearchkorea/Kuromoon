Chiharu Shiota Solo Exhibition 《Into the Light of Memory》

🗓️ Period: 2026-08-30 ~ 2027-02-21

World-renowned contemporary artist Chiharu Shiota's solo exhibition featuring new works in Daejeon.

Chiharu Shiota's solo exhibition, "Into the Light of Memory," presents a profound concept exploring the boundaries of presence and absence, memory and oblivion, through her overwhelming installation art using red threads. Visitors can immerse themselves in a visually striking and sensorially engaging artistic experience, encountering various installation works, including new pieces, across two venues: Heredium and Artsite Soje in Daejeon. The monumental spatial installations woven with red threads are the exhibition's signature elements and unmissable photo opportunities. Set within a modern and artistic environment, this exhibition is ideal for friends, couples, or individuals seeking deep inspiration and contemplative moments with art. Early bird ticket information is available, so it is recommended to book tickets in advance for a more convenient visit.
