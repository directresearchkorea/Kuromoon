Pop-up for Hirokazu Kore-eda's film 'Look Back'

This pop-up event celebrates the release of the live-action film 'Look Back,' which involved director Hirokazu Kore-eda in its original work. It offers a space to directly experience the film's atmosphere and can be visited by prior reservation.
