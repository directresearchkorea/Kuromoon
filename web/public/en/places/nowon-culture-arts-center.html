Nowon Culture & Arts Center

A cultural center in Nowon hosting impressionist art exhibitions and ballet festivals.

Nowon Culture & Arts Center is a comprehensive cultural space that offers visitors a rich array of artistic experiences through exhibitions of Impressionist masters and diverse ballet performances. At the Nowon Art Museum on the 4th floor, one can encounter beloved Impressionist works in the 'Impressionism, Brilliant Moments' exhibition, while the NBF:B Nowon Ballet Festival presents a broad spectrum of ballet art, from classical to contemporary. The 'Brilliant Moments' of the Impressionist exhibition and the special performances at the ballet festival are signature experiences not to be missed. This venue provides an excellent atmosphere for those seeking artistic inspiration or wishing to enjoy high-quality cultural life with family, friends, or partners.
