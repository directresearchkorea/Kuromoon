A spacious art/theme bakery cafe with ample parking in Yeongjongdo.

Starfive Cafe in Yeongjongdo is a large art/theme cafe boasting a unique concept where visitors can enjoy a variety of breads alongside their coffee, going beyond a simple coffee spot. Guests can experience a tranquil escape even on weekday afternoons in its spacious and pleasant environment, satisfying both visual and taste senses with fresh bread and aromatic coffee. The excellent combination of bread and coffee is highly recommended as a signature offering, perfect for a leisurely time. Its wide and comfortable interior atmosphere is ideal for solo contemplation or conversations with friends, making it a perfect spot for those seeking a small break from daily life. Furthermore, the cafe provides ample parking, ensuring convenience for visitors arriving by car.
