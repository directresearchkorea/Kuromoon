A unique themed restaurant and gallery cafe in Wanju, boasting a beautiful lake view.

Nestled by the lake in Soyang-myeon, Wanju, 'Ahwon' is a unique themed restaurant and gallery that embraces the Osungje Reservoir as its own backyard. Visitors can enjoy meals with a beautiful lake view, appreciate art in the gallery, and create special memories at the self-photo studio. The Osungje Lake view and the gallery space itself offer a signature experience, with the self-photo studio, known as a "profile picture hotspot," being a must-visit photo zone. This tranquil place, where art and gourmet dining meet amidst beautiful nature, is perfect for couples, families, and friends seeking a special outing. It is recommended to visit along with nearby attractions like Os Gallery, Soyang Hanok Village, and Doube Cafe, and planning for a less crowded time is advisable as weekends can be busy.
