Sarangbang

🔥 Over 23256 Naver blog reviews

A local cafe offering gluten-free options, various bingsu, and traditional teas.

'Sarangbang' in Buk-gu, Daegu, is a cozy neighborhood cafe especially beloved by those seeking gluten-free options. It serves as a true 'sarangbang' (gathering place) where diverse customers, from seniors to younger generations, comfortably visit and chat, proving its popularity with a continuous stream of guests. It is highly recommended not only for those who prefer gluten-free options but also for anyone wanting to taste a variety of beverages, including refreshing red bean bingsu and mango bingsu, warm jujube tea, and aromatic coffee. The red bean bingsu made with domestic red beans and fresh mango bingsu are particularly popular, and guests can also enjoy coffee with a choice of two different bean types, ensuring a satisfying experience.
