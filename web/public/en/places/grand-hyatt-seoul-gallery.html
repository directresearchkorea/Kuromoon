Luxury lounge cafe in the lobby of Grand Hyatt Seoul.

The Gallery Lounge, situated in the lobby of Grand Hyatt Seoul, is a luxurious art and theme cafe offering a relaxed atmosphere with an exotic ambiance. Visitors can enjoy the beautiful views of the hotel lobby, providing a unique experience as if they were abroad, and in summer, the signature apple mango bingsu is a highly popular menu item. Its elegant and sophisticated interior is perfect for special gatherings with friends or couples, as well as for enjoying quiet solo time. Three hours of parking support is provided, ensuring a convenient visit.
