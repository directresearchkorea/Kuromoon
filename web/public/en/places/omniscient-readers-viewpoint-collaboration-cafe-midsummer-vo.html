A collaboration cafe popup themed after the popular webtoon 'Omniscient Reader's Viewpoint'.

This 'Midsummer Voyage' collaboration cafe popup store, themed after the popular webtoon 'Omniscient Reader's Viewpoint', offers an immersive experience for fans in a special space reflecting the work's worldview. Visitors can enjoy various merchandise and collaboration menus, and notably, five special collaboration photo frames with Photoism allow them to capture unique memories. The 'Omniscient Reader's Viewpoint' special collaboration photo frames, exclusively available at Photoism, are a must-experience signature photo zone. This space is perfect for fans to share the webtoon's sensibility with friends or partners, and since it runs for a limited period from July 31 to August 17, 2026, at Goods Moment, it is advisable to check the visiting schedule in advance.
