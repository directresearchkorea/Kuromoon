Create your own tufting keyring at Mooi Atelier in Hongdae, a unique one-day class.

Mooi Atelier in Hongdae offers a unique one-day class where visitors can create their own personalized tufting keyrings. Guests can experience the joy of designing and crafting a one-of-a-kind keyring using various colored yarns and tools, fostering a sense of accomplishment. The self-made tufting keyring becomes a signature item reflecting personal style, and the charming studio provides lovely spots for commemorative photos. Its cozy and comfortable atmosphere makes it perfect for creating special memories with friends or partners, or for enjoying a focused solo crafting session.
