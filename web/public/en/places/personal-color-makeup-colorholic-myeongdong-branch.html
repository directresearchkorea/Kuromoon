Experience professional personal color analysis and customized makeup in Myeongdong.

Personal Color Makeup Colorholic Myeongdong Branch is a professional beauty space dedicated to helping individuals discover their unique colors for optimal beauty. Visitors can come without makeup, bringing their usual cosmetics, to receive expert diagnosis of their personal color and gain tailored makeup tips. A signature experience involves discovering the most flattering shades through detailed diagnoses like Winter Deep/Bright Cool Tone. This professional and clean environment is perfect for friends or couples seeking new styles, or for individuals to focus on self-care. For the best diagnosis, prepare your usual makeup palette, lipsticks, and blushes, and only apply colorless sunscreen before your visit.
