Indian home-style curry restaurant near Gyeongbokgung Station in Jongno.

Sajik-dong Geu Gage is an Indian home-style curry restaurant located near Gyeongbokgung Station in Jongno, offering a tranquil and relaxed atmosphere in Seochon's Sajik-dong. Visitors can enjoy delicious Indian home-style cuisine in its cozy indoor space and relaxed outdoor area, making for a special time. The deeply flavored curry and fragrant chai are highly recommended as must-try signature items. With its excellent and relaxed ambiance, it's perfect for dates with friends or partners, as well as for enjoying some solo time. The restaurant is regularly closed on Mondays and operates from Tuesday to Sunday, 11:30 AM to 8:00 PM, so please keep this in mind when planning your visit.
