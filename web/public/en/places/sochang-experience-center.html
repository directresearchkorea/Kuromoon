Unique center in Ganghwa-do offering Hanbok, traditional tea, and stamp experiences.

Sochang Experience Center is a unique cultural space in Ganghwa-do, themed around Sochang, a traditional textile. Visitors can experience a journey back in time by wearing beautiful Hanbok and enjoying traditional tea in a tranquil atmosphere. Hanbok wearing and traditional tea tasting are signature activities here, and a free stamp experience allows guests to create their own special souvenirs. With its retro and vintage Hanok ambiance, it's perfect for an unusual indoor date with friends or couples, or for families seeking an educational and enjoyable time.
