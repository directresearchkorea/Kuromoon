Rooftop art cafe in Seongsu, perfect after a walk in Seoul Forest.

Green Hall in Seongsu is an art/theme cafe perfect for a visit after a stroll through Seoul Forest, boasting unique spatial concepts like 'Green Hall' and 'Green Room'. The second-floor 'Green Room' offers a wide-open feel with large windows, providing a visual experience where visitors can relax and enjoy the natural ambiance of Seoul Forest. Known as a dessert hotspot, it offers various desserts and beverages, and the second-floor rooftop area serves as a great photo zone with Seongsu's beautiful scenery as a backdrop. Its bright and open interior makes it an ideal spot for friends or couples to unwind after a Seoul Forest date, and it's also suitable for enjoying some solo time. While no specific reservation or waiting tips are mentioned, it's advisable to consider that it might be crowded on weekends.
