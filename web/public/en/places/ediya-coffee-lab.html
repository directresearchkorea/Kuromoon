EDIYA COFFEE LAB

Large bakery cafe in Nonhyeon-dong, Gangnam, offering diverse coffee and pastries.

EDIYA COFFEE LAB distinguishes itself from regular EDIYA stores as a sophisticated art/theme cafe, embodying a coffee research lab concept to offer diverse specialty coffee experiences. Visitors can enjoy a wide selection of coffee, including brewing, espresso, and lab signature menus, alongside fresh bakery items in a spacious and comfortable setting. The highlight is the extensive coffee menu tailored to individual preferences and the fresh pastries available at the self-service bar. Its spacious and pleasant interior makes it ideal for gatherings with friends, romantic dates, or even solo relaxation, highly recommended for those seeking a comfortable visit without parking worries. Located in Nonhyeon-dong, Gangnam, it offers valet parking, making it easily accessible even for novice drivers.
