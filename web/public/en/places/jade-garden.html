Beautiful European-style arboretum in Chuncheon, Gangwon-do.

Jade Garden Arboretum, nestled in Chuncheon, Gangwon-do, is a nature-friendly space filled with European-style exotic gardens and diverse themed plants. Visitors can enjoy a peaceful retreat amidst nature, strolling along beautifully designed paths and admiring a variety of flowers and trees. While no specific signature menu is mentioned, the arboretum's picturesque gardens and sculptures throughout provide excellent photo opportunities. The serene atmosphere, harmonizing with lush nature, makes it an ideal destination for romantic dates, family outings, or solitary contemplation. Admission is available via on-site ticket purchase without prior reservation, and a spacious free parking lot ensures convenient access.
