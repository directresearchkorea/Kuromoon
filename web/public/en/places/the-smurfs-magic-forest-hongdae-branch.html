Smurf-themed media art escape room cafe in Hongdae.

Smurf Magic Forest Hongdae Branch is a unique media art cafe and an exciting mystery escape room experience, themed around the beloved Smurfs from childhood memories. Visitors can immerse themselves in the enchanting world of Smurfs, solving various missions alongside the cute characters and enjoying a visually captivating experience. After completing their quests, guests can relax with cafe offerings, and the entire Smurf-themed environment provides numerous unique photo opportunities. This spot is highly recommended for couples seeking an unusual indoor date or families planning a special weekend outing with elementary school-aged children.
