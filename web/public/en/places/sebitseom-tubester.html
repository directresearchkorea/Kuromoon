Sebitseom Tubester

🔥 Over 951 Naver blog reviews
✅ Recent status: 운영 중

Enjoy a water picnic on the Han River, taking in the night view and sunset.

Sebitseom Tubester offers a unique water picnic experience at Banpo Hangang Park, allowing visitors to fully enjoy Seoul's beautiful night view and sunset from the water. Guests can sit in a Tubester boat, feel the cool breeze of the Han River, and spend special moments in a romantic atmosphere, with the water picnic itself being the signature experience. It's perfect for capturing memorable photos against the stunning Han River backdrop, especially during sunset which creates the best photo zone. With its unique and romantic ambiance, it is highly recommended as a date course for couples or a special outing spot for friends.
