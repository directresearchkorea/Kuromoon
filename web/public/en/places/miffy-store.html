Miffy Store

✅ Recent status: 운영 중

Miffy-themed souvenir shop and cafe in Hwanglidan-gil, Gyeongju.

Located in Gyeongju's Hwanglidan-gil, Miffy Store is a charming souvenir shop and cafe themed around the beloved Miffy character. Visitors can browse and purchase a variety of Miffy merchandise, including exclusive limited-edition items like the Seokguram Miffy Edition. The store offers a delightful experience where guests can enjoy beverages alongside adorable Miffy-themed goods, providing both visual pleasure and a relaxing break. With its cozy and lovely ambiance, it's an ideal spot for friends or couples to create special memories, and a must-visit for any Miffy enthusiast. While on-site parking is not available, convenient public parking can be found near the Hwangnam-dong Ancient Tombs or within Hwanglidan-gil.
