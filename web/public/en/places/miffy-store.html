Miffy Store

✅ Recent status: 운영 중

Miffy-themed gift shop and cafe located in Gyeongju's Hwanglidan-gil.

Miffy Store, nestled in Gyeongju's Hwanglidan-gil, is a charming gift shop and cafe themed around the beloved Miffy character. Visitors can browse and purchase a wide array of Miffy merchandise, including exclusive Gyeongju-limited Seokguram Miffy edition goods. The limited-edition Seokguram Miffy items are a unique signature experience available only here, making them a must-have for Miffy fans. With its cute and cozy atmosphere, it's a delightful spot to enjoy time with Miffy, perfect for friends, couples, or even solo visitors looking for special souvenirs. Located at the end of Hwanglidan-gil, the store does not have its own parking, so it's recommended to use public parking lots near Hwangnam-dong Ancient Tombs.
