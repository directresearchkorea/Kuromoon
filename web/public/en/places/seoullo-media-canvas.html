Seoullo Media Canvas

✅ Recent status: 운영 중

Free media art exhibition at Seoul's major landmarks.

Seoullo Media Canvas is a unique platform showcasing media art across various prominent locations in Seoul, offering artistic inspiration within the city. Visitors can enjoy diverse visual experiences through media art videos displayed at places like Nodeul Island, Gwanghwamun, Haechimadang, and Ogansu Bridge. From 8 PM to 10 PM, the media canvas presents stunning works of light and video, offering a fantastic opportunity to capture special moments. This outdoor exhibition in the city center is perfect for friends and couples seeking a unique night out, or for individuals looking for a contemplative experience. It is free to visit without any prior reservation, making it an accessible and enjoyable cultural activity.
