Cafe Memorial

🔥 Over 26288 Naver blog reviews
✅ Recent status: 행사 중

Official Blue Archive themed cafe offering new experiences each season.

Cafe Memorial is the official themed cafe for the popular game 'Blue Archive,' offering fans a special experience with new concepts and themes each season. Visitors can enjoy menu items and merchandise themed around different student characters, immersing themselves in the game's world. Signature limited-edition menus and highly collectible merchandise are particularly popular among fans and are considered must-buy items. The space, designed to recreate the game's atmosphere, is perfect for friends or couples to share their fandom and create memories, and also suitable for solo visits to leisurely enjoy the theme. As it operates on a seasonal basis, it is recommended to check the official channels for the current theme and operating period before visiting.
