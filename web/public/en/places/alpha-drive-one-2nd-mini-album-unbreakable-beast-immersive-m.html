Immersive mission pop-up for idol group ALLYZ's 2nd mini album.

This immersive mission pop-up celebrates the release of idol group ALLYZ's second mini-album, 'UNBREAKABLE : 少年BEAST'. Visitors can engage in various missions within the pop-up to directly experience the album's worldview and capture memories at a special heart four-cut photo booth in collaboration with Double Lovers. The heart four-cut photo booth, a result of the collaboration with Double Lovers, is a must-visit signature photo zone for fans. Located in a trendy Seongsu spot, this pop-up is perfect for idol fans, friends, or couples seeking a unique date, offering a distinct atmosphere that allows full immersion into the album's concept. The experience is systematically organized from reservation to missions, so it's advisable to check relevant information before visiting.
