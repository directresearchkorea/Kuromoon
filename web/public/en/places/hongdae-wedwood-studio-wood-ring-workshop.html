Unique wood ring workshop in Hongdae where you can create your own custom ring.

Hongdae Wedwood Studio is a unique workshop offering a special memory of crafting your own wooden ring. Visitors can choose from various woods like maple and experience every step of ring making, from drilling holes to applying dyes. The signature outcome is a one-of-a-kind wooden ring reflecting your personality, with dyed wooden rings adding an extra special charm. This cozy and focused atmosphere is highly recommended for unique dates with partners, special activities with friends, or even for enjoying a solo immersive experience.
