Blue Ribbon-selected Swiss-style Western restaurant in Seochon.

La Suisse Seochon is a Swiss-concept dining restaurant in Seochon, selected by Blue Ribbon for 13 consecutive years, offering an exotic culinary experience. Visitors can enjoy unique Swiss dishes like Rösti, a Swiss-style potato pancake served on a hot stone plate, providing a special visual and gastronomic delight. Rösti, in particular, is a must-try signature dish here, offering a warm and distinctive experience. As a restaurant with a great ambiance in Seochon, it is recommended for romantic dates or special gatherings with friends, and it's also perfect for enjoying wine pairings. It's an ideal place for those looking to create special memories with exotic Swiss cuisine.
