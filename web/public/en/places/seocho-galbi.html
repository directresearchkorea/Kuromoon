Seocho Galbi: Retro-style charcoal grilled pork ribs in Jeonpo-dong.

Seocho Galbi is a charcoal-grilled pork rib restaurant in Jeonpo-dong, Busan, offering a rich retro ambiance. Visitors can enjoy delicious pork ribs grilled over charcoal, and a notably large jeon (Korean pancake) is a highly recommended side dish. Be sure to try the tender pork ribs paired with the crispy jeon for a complete experience. Its cozy atmosphere makes it perfect for dining with friends or loved ones. The restaurant is closed every Monday, and the last order is at 9:30 PM, so plan your visit accordingly.
