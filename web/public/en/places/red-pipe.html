Large brunch cafe with Han River view rooftop dining and a kids' room in Paju.

Red Pipe is a large brunch cafe in Paju offering Han River view rooftop dining, uniquely designed with a kids' room, making it ideal for family visitors. Guests can enjoy delicious brunch in a spacious and pleasant environment, while children can play safely in the dedicated kids' room. The signature offerings include a variety of fresh brunch menus, with the added charm of dining while overlooking the beautiful Han River. The cafe boasts a comfortable and warm atmosphere, highly recommended for family outings with children or those seeking a weekday morning drive destination. Ample parking is available directly in front of the store, with guidance to alternative parking if the main lot is full.
