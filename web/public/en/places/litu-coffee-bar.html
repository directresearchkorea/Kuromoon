A concept cafe near Sinpyeongso Park in Gijang, Busan, famous for Basque cheesecake.

Litu Coffee Bar, nestled near Sinpyeongso Park in Gijang, Busan, is a concept cafe offering a tranquil atmosphere. It is particularly renowned for its smooth and rich Basque cheesecake, providing a delightful experience for dessert lovers with its variety of options. Recommended for those seeking a unique coffee experience combined with a leisurely stroll, as it boasts a hidden charm requiring a short walk from the breakwater parking area. Visitors can enjoy the signature Basque cheesecake and the allure of an espresso bar; it's advisable to check the differing weekday and weekend operating hours before visiting.
