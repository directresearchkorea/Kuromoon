Bakery cafe in Ikseon-dong offering various breads and brunch menus.

Standard Bread Ikseon is a bakery cafe located in Ikseon-dong, offering a variety of breads, French toast, and brunch menus. It provides a unique selection compared to typical bakeries and is conveniently situated in the same building as the famous Oncheonjip in Ikseon-dong.
