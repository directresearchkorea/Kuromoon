Carin X Taku Bannai Special Exhibition - Moments on the Road

A cultural exhibition by fashion brand Carin and Taku Bannai.

The Carin X Taku Bannai Special Exhibition 「Moments on the Road」 is a unique cultural exhibition expanding a fashion brand's seasonal theme through photography and space. This exhibition captures small moments found on the road and the emotions of travel, offering visitors a visual delight. Guests can gain special inspiration through artworks that artistically express various travel moments. Held in Seongsu, this exhibition provides a new experience for those who love fashion and art, making it perfect for visiting with friends or a partner.
