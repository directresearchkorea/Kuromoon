Art/theme cafe famous for its pies near Seoul Station.

Focal Point, located near Seoul Station, is an art/theme cafe renowned for its wide variety of pies. The low-sugar Chungju apple pie is particularly popular, and it operates daily from 7:30 AM until late, making it convenient for visitors.
