A value-for-money omakase sushi restaurant near Seongsu Station.

Omakase Osai Sushi Seongsu Branch is a highly-rated omakase sushi restaurant known for its excellent value, nestled in an alley near Seongsu Station. Visitors can enjoy the unique experience of discovering this hidden gem, located a short walk from Seongsu Station Exit 4. The restaurant offers a diverse and meticulously prepared sushi course using fresh ingredients, providing a delightful culinary journey at a reasonable price. With its clean and cozy ambiance, it's an ideal spot for a special meal with friends or a romantic date, and also welcoming for solo diners. To find it, exit Seongsu Station at Exit 4, walk past the Seven-Eleven, and then turn into the alley.
