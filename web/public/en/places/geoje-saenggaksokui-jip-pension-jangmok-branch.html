A spacious and cost-effective Geoje pension with a great ocean view.

Geoje Saenggaksokui Jip Pension Jangmok Branch is a pension that boasts a beautiful ocean view, offering visitors a comfortable and cost-effective retreat. Guests can enjoy spacious rooms with panoramic sea views, spending leisurely time, and can also link their stay with a healing experience on Isudo Island. The stunning ocean view is a signature experience here, allowing guests to truly relax while gazing at the sea from their rooms. With an overall cozy and peaceful atmosphere, it is perfect for couples, families, and even those seeking quiet solo healing. Staying for multiple nights on weekdays offers a more reasonable price, and for Isudo visits, utilizing the pension owner's pickup service is convenient.
