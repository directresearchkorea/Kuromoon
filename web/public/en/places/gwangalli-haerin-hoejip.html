Raw fish restaurant with Gwangandaegyo Bridge view and private rooms.

Gwangalli Haerin Hoejip is a private room raw fish restaurant boasting a fantastic view that captures the Gwangandaegyo Bridge and the beautiful Gwangalli Beach at a glance. Visitors can enjoy fresh seasonal raw fish while admiring the refreshing sea view outside the window and the dazzling nightscape of Gwangandaegyo Bridge. A signature experience here is savoring fresh seafood dishes and capturing memorable photos against the backdrop of the expansive ocean view. The clean and sophisticated private rooms are perfect for romantic dates, precious family gatherings, or important business meals. Public parking support is available, ensuring convenient access for guests.
