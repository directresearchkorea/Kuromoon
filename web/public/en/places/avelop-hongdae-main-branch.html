A charming bakery cafe near Hongdae Station with a vintage house vibe.

Avelop Hongdae Main Branch is a bakery cafe that offers a unique sensibility, having been renovated from an old house located between Hapjeong and Hongdae Entrance stations. Visitors can enjoy a leisurely time with friends in a cozy and satisfying atmosphere from the moment they step inside. The cinnamon latte is noted as a must-try, alongside a variety of bakery items and beverages such as bagels, croissants, and matcha latte, catering to diverse tastes. The warm and emotional interior, characteristic of a renovated old house, provides a perfect space for dates with friends or for those seeking a comfortable retreat.
