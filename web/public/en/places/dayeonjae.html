Experience traditional tea ceremony in a serene hanok in Sejong.

Dayeonjae is a concept dining space in a tranquil hanok in Sejong Special Self-Governing City, offering a deep dive into Korea's traditional tea ceremony culture. Visitors can learn the art of brewing tea in the beautiful ambiance of a hanok, enjoying a peaceful time that satisfies all senses. Here, one can savor meticulously prepared traditional teas and refreshments, fully experiencing the beauty of Korea. Its serene and calm atmosphere is perfect for a special date with friends or loved ones, as well as for quiet contemplation alone.
