Famous vegan bakery in Seongsu, popular for salt bread and chocolate baguette.

Bon Noel Seongsu Branch is a renowned vegan bakery located in Seongdong-gu, Seoul, which has been loved for a long time, appearing on numerous broadcasts such as Cheonhajebbang, Master of Life, and Saengsaengjeongbo. This place particularly stands out for offering healthy vegan bread, attracting many visitors with its fresh and delicious baked goods. Due to its popularity, there can be a waiting line even on weekday afternoons, so it's advisable to keep this in mind when visiting, making it an excellent stop for a bread pilgrimage in Seongsu-dong. The signature salt bread and chocolate baguette are highly recommended must-try items, allowing visitors to experience a wide variety of vegan breads.
