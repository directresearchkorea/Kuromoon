Jangheung Gyegokjip

🔥 Over 104 Naver blog reviews
✅ Recent status: 운영 중

Enjoy chicken soup and water play in a valley, pet-friendly restaurant.

Jangheung Gyegokjip offers a unique dining concept where visitors can enjoy traditional Korean chicken soup (baeksuk) amidst the beautiful natural scenery of Jangheung Valley in Yangju. Guests can immerse their feet in the cool valley stream and enjoy water play while dining, providing a special visual and sensory experience, especially during the summer. The signature dish here is baeksuk, best enjoyed with the ambiance of the valley, featuring a rich and deep flavor from fresh ingredients. With its nature-friendly atmosphere, it is perfect for families, friends, couples, and even those with pets to spend a relaxing and enjoyable time.
