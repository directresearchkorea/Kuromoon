Pop-up store for the popular webtoon '44th Period Survival Class'.

This special pop-up store allows visitors to directly experience the world of the popular webtoon '44th Period Survival Class'. Guests can enjoy various visual delights in spaces recreating scenes from the webtoon and discover limited edition merchandise. The photo zones, featuring key characters and symbols from the webtoon, offer a must-try signature experience. It's perfect for friends or couples who appreciate the webtoon's charm hidden behind its cute art style, providing a fun time together. Located at Goods Moment in Hongdae, parking is available for convenient visits.
