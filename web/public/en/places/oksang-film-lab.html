Oksang Film Lab

🔥 Over 267 Naver blog reviews

Specialized film development and scanning lab for film cameras.

Oksang Film Lab is a specialized development center for those who cherish the analog sensibility of film cameras, helping to preserve precious moments captured on film. Here, visitors can convert their analog photos into digital files through film development and scanning services, with film printing also available to preserve memories in various ways. The lab operates an unmanned drop-off system, allowing for convenient film submission, and developed films can be received via delivery or in-person pickup. It's a special place that allows all film camera enthusiasts to rediscover analog charm, perfect for preserving personal memories or visiting with friends for a new experience. Please note that film development can take over a week after submission, so it's advisable to plan your visit with ample time and enjoy the anticipation.
