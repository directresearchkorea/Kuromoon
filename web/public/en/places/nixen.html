Nixen, a rooftop concept cafe located in Namyangju.

'Nixen' is an attractive concept cafe with a rooftop, located in Namyangju, Gyeonggi-do. It's perfect for creating cherished memories with loved ones or enjoying solitary relaxation in the special ambiance of its beautiful rooftop space. Beyond a variety of desserts and beverages, the pleasant environment and service contribute to high visitor satisfaction.
