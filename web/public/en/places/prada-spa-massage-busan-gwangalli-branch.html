Enjoy a relaxing massage at Prada Spa in Gwangalli with a view of the beach.

Prada Spa Massage Busan Gwangalli Branch offers a premium beauty and wellness experience set against the stunning backdrop of Gwangalli Beach. Visitors can indulge in a refreshing massage while enjoying panoramic views of the Gwangalli sea, providing a unique opportunity to soothe both body and mind. The spa offers a variety of programs, including traditional Thai massage and aromatic oil massage, allowing guests to choose according to their preferences, and is popular for couple massages. With its comfortable and cozy ambiance, it's an ideal spot for a romantic date with a loved one, a relaxing time with friends, or a peaceful solo retreat. Operating daily from 11 AM to midnight, it is recommended to visit during your leisure time when exploring Gwangalli Beach for a truly comfortable massage experience.
