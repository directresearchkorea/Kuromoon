Riverside Italian restaurant with a Han River view in Miumnaru.

Romaok Riverside is an Italian restaurant uniquely situated along the Han River, offering a concept dining experience where guests can fully enjoy beautiful river views. Visitors can spend leisurely time admiring the expansive Han River scenery, truly experiencing the charm of the Miumnaru riverside. The restaurant's signature dish is the highly praised squid ink risotto, known for its rich flavor, making it a must-try delicacy. With its romantic ambiance provided by the Han River view, it is strongly recommended for dates with loved ones or special family dining occasions. Given its popularity, following the success of its Magok and Dogok branches, it is advisable to check for availability before visiting.
