Traditional tea ceremony experience in Jeonju Hanok Village.

Gyodong Dawon is a traditional tea ceremony experience space located in Jeonju Hanok Village, offering a deep dive into Korean tea culture amidst the serene atmosphere of a traditional Hanok. Visitors can calmly learn tea etiquette in the beautiful Hanok courtyard and neat indoor spaces, savoring the aroma and taste of various traditional teas for a truly sensory experience. The signature offering is the hands-on tea ceremony set, and the classic Hanok backdrop provides excellent photo opportunities. Its calm and relaxed ambiance is perfect for creating special memories with friends or loved ones, as well as for enjoying quiet contemplation alone. It is advisable to check the availability and schedule of tea ceremonies in advance before visiting.
