A hidden gem wine bar with a great atmosphere near Ttukdo Market in Seongsu.

Kkumgyeol Seongsu Branch is an art/theme wine bar with a hidden gem atmosphere nestled within Ttukdo Market. Here, visitors can enjoy a special time with wine, and on the last Saturday of every month, live music performances offer an even richer sensory experience. As a wine bar, it boasts a diverse selection of wines, providing a comfortable and emotional space to relax. This spot is highly recommended for couples seeking a romantic date night or friends looking for a stylish gathering in Seongsu, and for those interested in the live music, planning your visit for the last Saturday of the month is advisable.
