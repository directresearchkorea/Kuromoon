One-day glass craft workshop in Seongsu-dong.

Soranhan Workshop in Seongsu-dong offers a unique experience where visitors can create their own special glass art through one-day classes. Here, you can take home your own unique glass artwork, making it a precious souvenir along with cherished memories. It's an ideal place to find artistic inspiration in a cozy and focused atmosphere even amidst the bustling city, highly recommended for friends or couples looking for a unique date, or individuals seeking to discover a new hobby.
