A 20-year-old specialty steamed bun bakery cafe next to Gomso Salt Farm in Buan.

Seulji Jjinpangso, nestled beside Buan's Gomso Salt Farm, is a bakery cafe specializing in steamed buns with a 20-year legacy, embodying a unique identity intertwined with the local landmark. Visitors can indulge in a variety of freshly prepared steamed buns and bakery items, enjoying the visual delight of the beautiful Gomso Salt Farm scenery. The steamed buns, boasting a two-decade history, are the signature menu item here, a must-try dessert for anyone on a bakery tour. With its cozy and comfortable interior, it's an ideal spot for friends, couples, or families to spend leisurely time, or for individuals seeking a sweet and peaceful break. Operating year-round, the cafe offers convenient parking both at its building and across the road at Gomso Salt Farm.
