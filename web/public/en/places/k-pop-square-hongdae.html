IDLE's MININI Baby Pop-up Store at K-Pop Square in Hongdae.

The IDLE MININI Baby Pop-up Store, held at K-Pop Square in Hongdae, offers a special experience for fans, allowing them to encounter adorable MININI Baby characters. Strategically located in the bustling heart of Hongdae, it boasts excellent accessibility and provides visitors with a delightful array of merchandise and captivating photo zones. Particularly popular are the limited edition MININI Baby-related props and souvenirs, making them highly sought after collector's items. Operating daily from 11 AM to 10 PM, it offers ample time for a leisurely visit.
