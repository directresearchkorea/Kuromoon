Balvenie x Daniel Arsham limited edition whisky pop-up.

The Balvenie, a renowned single malt whisky brand, collaborates with world-renowned contemporary artist Daniel Arsham to present the 'Dawn of Our Spirit' limited edition collection pop-up exhibition. Visitors can visually experience the unique harmony of traditional handcrafted single malt whisky and contemporary art, encountering the special limited edition collection firsthand. The Balvenie Millstone limited edition collection, in particular, will be an unmissable signature item for whisky enthusiasts. This space offers a unique experience for friends, couples, or individuals who appreciate whisky and art and seek a special time.
