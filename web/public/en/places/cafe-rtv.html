A concept cafe by AI tech company RTV, located near Yangcheonhyanggyo Station.

Cafe RTV began as an in-house cafe for the AI technology company Realtime Visual (RTV), embodying the unique concept of 'loose connections'. It seeks to find a balance between intimacy and distance within sustainable bonds, reflecting a thoughtful theme. Located on the first floor of the Nollaum building, past the Gyeomjae Jeongseon Art Museum, its distinctive entrance might initially suggest it's closed, sparking visitor curiosity. This cafe, with its emotional atmosphere, is recommended for friends or couples eager to explore a space with a novel concept.
