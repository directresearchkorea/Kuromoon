A unique perfume workshop in Seongsu-dong, Seoul Forest, to create your own scent.

Laoc Seongsu is a unique perfume workshop located in Seoul Forest, Seongsu, offering visitors the special experience of creating their own one-of-a-kind fragrance. Guests can enjoy the process of discovering their unique scent by sampling various fragrance notes in a clean and atmospheric wood-toned interior. The 30ml perfume class is a signature experience at Laoc, where participants can craft a personalized perfume with the guidance of an expert perfumer. The calm and cozy wood-toned interior provides a perfect setting for a date with a loved one, creating special memories with friends, or exploring a new hobby during solo time.
