Sleep No More Seoul

🔥 Over 2477 Naver blog reviews
✅ Recent status: 운영 중

Immersive unique experience performance at Maekitan Hotel

Sleep No More Seoul is a unique immersive performance held in the space of Maekitan Hotel, which transformed the entire Chungmuro Daehan Cinema. Unlike traditional musicals, there are no fixed seats or predetermined paths; audiences freely move and explore the story in their own way. The pre-opening generated intense competition for tickets, highlighting its high anticipation.
