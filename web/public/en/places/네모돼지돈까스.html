Ulsan Jung-gu restaurant specializing in pork cutlets and salmon dishes.

Located on Munhwa-ui-geori in Jung-gu, Ulsan, Nemo Pork Cutlet is a concept dining spot offering both black pork cutlets and fresh salmon dishes. Guests can enjoy snow cheese pork cutlets made with 100% natural cheese, as well as various salmon items like Nemo salmon sushi and salmon donburi.
