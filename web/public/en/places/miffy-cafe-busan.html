Miffy-themed cafe and goods store in Yeongdo, Busan.

Miffy Cafe Busan, nestled in Yeongdo, Busan, is a charming space uniquely themed around the beloved Miffy character. Visitors can immerse themselves in the adorable Miffy-filled interior while enjoying drinks and desserts, and delight in browsing and purchasing a diverse range of Miffy merchandise. This cafe is a must-visit for Miffy fans, offering various goods and numerous visually appealing spots perfect for photos. With its cute and cozy ambiance, it's an ideal destination for friends or couples looking to create special memories. Conveniently, ample free parking is provided, with staff available to guide visitors.
