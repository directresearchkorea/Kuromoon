Changuk Tteok pop-up at The Hyundai Seoul, famous for pumpkin chapssaltteok.

The Changuk Tteok pop-up at The Hyundai Seoul brings this popular SNS-famous tteok brand to the capital city. Visitors can taste and purchase various types of tteok, especially the highly praised chewy pumpkin chapssaltteok. The signature pumpkin chapssaltteok is noted for being 100 times chewier and more delicious, becoming even softer at room temperature. Operating as a pop-up store, it's an excellent spot for friends or family who enjoy traditional Korean rice cakes to discover unique treats. Due to its popularity, with people lining up for "open run," it's advisable to allow ample time for your visit.
