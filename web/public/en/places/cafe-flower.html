A large botanical garden-themed cafe, famous for its Hwang Cheese Cookie.

Cafe & Flower is an art/theme cafe designed with a botanical garden concept, featuring flowers and plants throughout its spacious interior. Visitors can relax in a comfortable, large space, enjoying the lush, plant-filled ambiance reminiscent of a greenhouse. The Hwang Cheese Cookie, noted for its delightful Ppo-tto cracker flavor, is a highly recommended signature item to pair with your coffee. Its large and pleasant atmosphere makes it an ideal spot for friends, couples, and families seeking a relaxing outing. Convenient parking facilities are available, making it easy for guests to visit by car.
