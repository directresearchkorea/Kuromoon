Popular Mexican restaurant in Seongsu, famous for tacos and fajitas.

Taco Tuesday Seongsu Main Branch is a popular Mexican dining spot in Seongsu-dong, offering a variety of authentic Mexican dishes. It's so popular that people often wait in line, and its tacos and classic fajitas, made with fresh ingredients, are exceptional. Located near Ttukseom Station, it offers great accessibility and is an ideal place to enjoy a delicious meal in an exotic atmosphere with friends or loved ones. The Classic Fajita and Chimichanga are highly recommended signature dishes by visitors, promising a hearty and satisfying dining experience.
