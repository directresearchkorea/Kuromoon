A stylish LP bar near Ttukseom Station in Seongsu.

Move On Seongsu is an LP bar quietly nestled on the second floor of a building near Ttukseom Station in Seongsu, filled with sophisticated music and a cozy ambiance. Its proximity to Ttukseom Station offers good accessibility, and its quiet second-floor location allows for a relaxed time away from the hustle and bustle.
