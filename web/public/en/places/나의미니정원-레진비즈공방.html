Resin art and bead craft workshop in Yeonnam-dong.

My Mini Garden Resin Bead Workshop is a unique craft experience studio located in Yeonnam-dong, Mapo-gu, Seoul. Visitors can create special jewelry pieces through resin art and bead crafting, utilizing various materials such as Czech beads, crystals, and mother-of-pearl. The workshop offers creative experiences, having been selected for a monthly creative workshop exhibition.
