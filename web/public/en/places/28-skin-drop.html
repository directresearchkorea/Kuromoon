Beauty workshop offering customized cosmetics and skincare products.

28 Skin Drop is a customized cosmetics workshop offering optimal solutions tailored to individual skin concerns. Visitors can experience and choose from a variety of functional skincare products, including those with over 90% pure liposomal glutathione, sunscreens providing an instant cooling effect, and EGF ampoules and calming creams that help strengthen the skin barrier. The powerful cooling sunscreen, suitable even for sensitive skin, and high-purity glutathione products are notable signature items to look out for. This place is recommended for anyone interested in cultivating healthy and firm skin, especially those looking to maintain their skin condition during dry seasonal changes or in arid indoor environments.
