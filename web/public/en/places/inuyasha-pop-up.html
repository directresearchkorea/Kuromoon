Inuyasha pop-up store opens at AK Plaza Hongdae.

The Inuyasha Pop-up Store, opening for the first time in Korea, offers a unique opportunity to directly experience the world of the popular anime Inuyasha. Visitors can browse and purchase a variety of merchandise, with exclusive early access to rare items like the pre-released long mouse pad. Must-have signature items include the Inuyasha MD goods, which are being unveiled for the first time in Korea, especially the pre-released long mouse pad. This space provides Inuyasha fans with the joy of reliving memories and collecting new merchandise, making it perfect for visiting with friends or a partner for a special time. The pop-up store is held at AK Plaza Hongdae 4F LIMITION and simultaneously at AK Plaza Suwon, so it's advisable to check the schedule before visiting.
