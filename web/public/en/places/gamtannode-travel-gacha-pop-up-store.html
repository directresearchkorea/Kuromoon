Gamtannode Travel Gacha pop-up store by Gangwon Tourism Foundation in Seongsu-dong.

The 'Gamtannode Travel Gacha Pop-up Store,' hosted by the Gangwon Tourism Foundation, offers a unique concept designed to promote the diverse charms of Gangwon-do and provide visitors with a special travel experience. Here, guests can engage in an unusual activity by drawing various travel products or souvenirs related to Gangwon-do through an exciting travel gacha. The 'travel gacha' itself is the signature experience, offering great fun and anticipation for what Gangwon treasure will be revealed. This vibrant pop-up is highly recommended for friends, couples, or families interested in Gangwon-do travel or seeking new experiences, promising enjoyable memories in a lively atmosphere. Scheduled to take place in Seongsu-dong in August 2026, it's advisable to check the exact dates before planning your visit.
