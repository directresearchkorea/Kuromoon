Hapjeong Station's dining spot for wine pairing, teppanyaki, and aged sashimi.

Lala & K Dining, located near Hapjeong Station, boasts a unique concept as a wine pairing specialist where guests can also enjoy teppanyaki and aged sashimi. Visitors can indulge in a refined culinary experience at elegantly set tables within a subtle and cozy ambiance, savoring a diverse menu and the rich flavors of the Lala Kase course. The signature offerings include teppanyaki and aged sashimi, perfectly optimized for wine pairing, alongside the chef-curated Lala Kase course. Its subtle and cozy interior is ideal for romantic dates or special gatherings with friends, with group seating also available to accommodate various types of visitors. Given its excellent accessibility from Hapjeong Station and superb atmosphere, this popular spot requires advance reservations.
