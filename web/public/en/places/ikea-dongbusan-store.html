Spacious restaurant within IKEA Dongbusan store

Located within IKEA Dongbusan, this restaurant offers a unique concept dining experience where visitors can combine shopping with culinary delights. Guests can enjoy a comfortable break in a spacious and pleasant environment, savoring a variety of Swedish and local dishes. IKEA's signature meatballs and salmon dishes are particularly popular and highly recommended for a taste of their classic offerings. It's an ideal spot for families, friends, or couples to dine after shopping, providing a relaxed atmosphere thanks to its expansive layout. To avoid crowds, especially on weekends and holidays, it is advisable to visit during off-peak dining hours.
