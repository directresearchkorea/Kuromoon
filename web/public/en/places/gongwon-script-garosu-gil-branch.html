A large cafe in Garosu-gil where you can enjoy LP music and desserts.

Gongwon Script Garosu-gil Branch is a unique art/theme cafe where visitors can enjoy a relaxing time listening to LP music. Spanning from the basement to the third floor, this spacious and pleasant venue offers a special visual and auditory experience, allowing guests to select and listen to various LPs. The fresh mango bingsu and diverse dessert menu are signature items here, making them a must-try during your visit. With its stylish interior and comfortable atmosphere, it's perfect for dates with friends or couples, as well as for enjoying some solo time. While conveniently located near Sinsa Station Exit 8, please note that parking requires utilizing nearby public parking lots.
