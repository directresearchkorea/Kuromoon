Jamsil LAoC Perfume Workshop

🔥 Over 1988 Naver blog reviews
✅ Recent status: 운영 중

Jamsil perfume workshop featured in Transit Love, offering one-day perfume making classes.

Jamsil LAoC Perfume Workshop, featured in the popular dating show 'Transit Love,' offers a unique experience where visitors can create their own one-of-a-kind perfume. Through various programs like the TAP Perfume Class and LAoC Reserve Blending Class, guests can combine and sample different fragrances with the help of professional perfumers to discover their signature scent. Given its appearance on 'Transit Love,' creating scents inspired by the show or crafting couple perfumes are particularly popular activities here. The classes, held in a cozy and professional atmosphere, are perfect for a unique indoor date with a partner, creating special memories with friends, or discovering a new hobby during solo time. Located in the basement next to a CU, it's advisable to check directions beforehand; operating daily from 12:00 to 20:00, it's recommended to confirm class times and make reservations prior to your visit.
