Olive Young N Seongsu Flagship pop-up store and O-Lens event.

The Olive Young N Seongsu Flagship is a special pop-up store event linked with various Olive Young branches in Seongsu-dong, offering visitors a unique beauty experience. Guests can explore different locations through a check-in rally and receive gifts like Bring Green lip essence. Additionally, the O-Lens Seongsu Station branch provides a free 2-piece sample event for one-day lenses to Olive Young Seongsu customers, enhancing the overall benefits. Recommended as a vibrant date course in Seongsu-dong, it's perfect for friends, couples, or solo visitors looking to enjoy beauty shopping and special events.
