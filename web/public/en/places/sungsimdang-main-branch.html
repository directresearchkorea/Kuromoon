Sungsimdang Main Branch, a representative bakery cafe in Daejeon.

Sungsimdang Main Branch is a renowned bakery cafe, celebrated as a specialty of Daejeon. It is particularly famous for its wide array of freshly baked breads and cakes, with seasonal limited-edition 'Siru' cakes like Mango Siru and Saenggyul Siru being highly popular among visitors. Conveniently located just a 2-minute walk from Jungangno Station Exit 2, its excellent accessibility makes it a must-visit destination when traveling to Daejeon. This is the perfect place to complete your culinary experience in Daejeon by savoring fresh bread and unique cakes.
