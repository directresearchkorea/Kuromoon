Laka and Olive Young's beauty pop-up store in Seongsu.

This beauty pop-up store, a collaboration between Laka and Olive Young in Seongsu, offers a space to experience the latest beauty trends. Located on the 2nd floor pop-up zone of Olive Young N Seongsu, visitors can try Laka products and enjoy various benefits, including limited-time opening events and mini tint gifts for purchasers. The special coupon available upon adding Laka as a KakaoTalk friend and the mini tint given to purchasing customers are must-not-miss signature benefits. With its trendy and vibrant atmosphere, it's perfect for friends to explore new beauty products and enjoy special offers, highly recommended for beauty enthusiasts. The pop-up runs for a limited period from August 1st to August 29th, 2026, so it's advisable to check the schedule before visiting.
