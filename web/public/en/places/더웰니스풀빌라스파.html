Private wellness pool villa & spa in Gapyeong

The Wellness Pool Villa & Spa offers a private wellness spa pool villa experience with a jungle view, reminiscent of Ubud, Bali. Guests can enjoy complete relaxation in their individual pool villas and spend serene, peaceful moments at the jungle-view spa. Rooms 1, 3, and 5 are particularly recommended for their enhanced privacy, and the Titik Dua Spa facility provides a special wellness experience. With duplex structured rooms, it's suitable for families, while offering a romantic retreat for couples and honeymooners. Complimentary parking is available for convenience, and guests with children should be mindful of safety in duplex rooms.
