Premium Korean beef course dining, ideal for family meetings and business events.

Modou Gwanghwamun is a premium Korean beef course dining restaurant in Gwanghwamun, Seoul, conceptualized for important occasions like family meetings and business gatherings. Visitors can enjoy private dining in elegant room settings, experiencing great satisfaction from the ambiance, service, and space provided. The must-try signature menu is the meticulously prepared Korean beef course, designed to enhance any special occasion. With its luxurious interior and attentive service, this venue is highly recommended for formal gatherings such as family meetings, business dinners, and other significant events. Located in Gwanghwamun Twin Tree Tower, it offers excellent accessibility, and it is advisable to plan ahead for a relaxed visit, especially for important meals.
