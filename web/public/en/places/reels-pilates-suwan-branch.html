A professional studio in Suwan District where you can start healthy exercise habits through Pilates.

Reels Pilates Suwan Branch is a professional Pilates studio in Gwangju's Suwan District, dedicated to helping individuals embark on a healthy exercise journey. Visitors can engage in systematic and effective apparatus Pilates sessions, offering an excellent option for those contemplating starting a new workout routine. The studio specializes in various apparatus Pilates classes, focusing on improving body balance and strengthening muscles, with the added convenience of accepting Onnuri gift certificates for payment. With its clean and professional atmosphere, it is highly recommended for individuals seeking a healthy lifestyle or beginners looking to start their fitness journey. Class reservations can be conveniently made for the following week's sessions via their app starting from the previous Saturday.
