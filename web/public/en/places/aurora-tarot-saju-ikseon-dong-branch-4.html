Unique themed cafe in Ikseon-dong offering tarot, saju, and Western astrology.

Aurora Tarot & Saju Ikseon-dong Branch 4, nestled in the alleys of Ikseon-dong, is a unique themed cafe where visitors can experience Western astrology, tarot, and saju all in one place. Guests can enjoy a special time listening to intriguing stories about their fate and future from professional consultants. The distinctive feature here is the ability to consult saju, Western astrology, and tarot simultaneously, offering diverse ways to explore oneself. With its mystical ambiance, it's an ideal spot for friends or couples seeking a unique date experience, or for individuals looking for a moment of deep introspection.
