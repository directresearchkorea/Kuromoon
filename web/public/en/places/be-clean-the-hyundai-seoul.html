A beauty multi-brand store located on B2F of The Hyundai Seoul.

Be Clean, located on the B2F of The Hyundai Seoul, is a beauty multi-brand store offering a curated selection of various beauty products. Visitors can explore and purchase popular items, including the PAU Cosmetics Glow Pumpkin series, experiencing them firsthand. Be sure to check out the PAU products, often available at a lower price than online, and don't miss the Glow Pumpkin line. Situated within a modern department store, it provides a pleasant shopping environment, ideal for friends or couples looking to discover new beauty items. It offers excellent accessibility via an underground passage from Yeouido Station and provides convenient parking for visitors.
