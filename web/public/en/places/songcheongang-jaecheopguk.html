Songcheongang Jaecheopguk

🔥 Over 261 Naver blog reviews
✅ Recent status: 운영 중

Local's favorite clam soup restaurant by Songcheongang River in Yeongdeok.

Songcheongang Jaecheopguk, nestled by the Songcheongang River in Yeongdeok, offers a unique local dining experience, surprising visitors with fresh clam dishes not typically associated with the region. Guests can enjoy their meal while gazing at the flowing river from window seats, creating a refreshing and peaceful dining atmosphere. The signature dishes include the refreshing and deeply flavored Jaecheopguk (clam soup) and chewy Jaecheop Sujebi (clam hand-pulled dough soup), both highly recommended by local residents. This spot is perfect for families, friends, or couples seeking a relaxed meal amidst Yeongdeok's natural beauty, and it's an ideal choice for travelers looking for authentic local cuisine.
