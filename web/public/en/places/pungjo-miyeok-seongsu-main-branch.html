A Seongsu restaurant offering warm seaweed soup and sea squirt bibimbap like home-cooked meals.

Pungjo Miyeok Seongsu Main Branch is a dining space concept that offers warm and hearty home-style meals. Here, visitors can experience a comfortable and satisfying meal, tasting deep and rich seaweed soup and fresh sea squirt bibimbap. The rich seaweed soup, perfect for a hearty meal before or after visiting pop-ups, and the unique sea squirt bibimbap are must-try signature dishes. It's an ideal spot to enjoy healthy Korean food in a warm and neat atmosphere, whether with friends, a partner, or for some solo time.
