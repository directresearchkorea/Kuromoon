Shugo Chara! x Popable collaboration cafe featuring character menus and merchandise.

This is a special collaboration cafe where Shugo Chara! and Popable have teamed up to create a unique theme based on the anime characters. Must-have signature items for fans include limited edition goods such as character cards, snapshot cards, and a total of five types of tapestries. Recommended for fans who wish to relive the nostalgia of 'Shugo Chara!' or for friends and couples seeking a special character experience, it offers a charming and lively atmosphere perfect for creating memories.
