Yeongjongdo cafe themed around Beethoven's love for coffee.

Beethoven House in Yeongjongdo is an art/theme cafe inspired by the fascinating fact that the great composer Beethoven was an avid coffee lover. Visitors can enjoy specialty coffee and delicious bread, learning about Beethoven's coffee passion, and also experience a unique time listening to classical music in the accompanying music hall. The specialty coffee, inspired by the story that Beethoven spent a quarter of his income on coffee, is a must-try signature item here. Its unique atmosphere, combining music and coffee, makes it perfect for those planning an unusual date or gathering with friends in Yeongjongdo.
