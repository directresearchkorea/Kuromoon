A Seomyeon Korean restaurant offering warm, home-style meals.

Bapiboyak 65 is a concept dining spot in Seomyeon where you can enjoy warm and neat home-style Korean food. Visitors can savor a hearty and satisfying meal with various Korean dishes, including grilled mackerel set meals and spicy pork set meals. The grilled mackerel, in particular, is highlighted as a memorable signature menu item. With a comfortable and cozy atmosphere perfect for visiting with family, it is highly recommended for those longing for home-cooked meals or planning a family outing. Free parking is available at Seomyeon Yurim The Blue 2 Officetel, making it convenient to visit.
