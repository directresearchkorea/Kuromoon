A clean and cozy salt bread specialty cafe located in Aewol, Jeju.

Pickup Cafe Aewol Branch is a newly opened salt bread specialty cafe in Aewol, Jeju, known for its clean and modern ambiance. Visitors can easily find it on Aewol Cafe Street and enjoy a relaxing time with freshly baked, delicious salt bread. The cafe's signature salt bread is highly recommended as a must-try dessert among Jeju tourists, praised for its exquisite taste and aroma. Its sophisticated interior makes it a perfect spot for dates with friends or partners, or for a quiet solo retreat. Conveniently, parking at the Kakao T Aewol Cafe Street parking lot offers 1 hour of free support.
