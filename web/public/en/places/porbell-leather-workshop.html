Leather craft workshop in Hongdae offering unique hands-on experiences.

Porbell Leather Workshop in Hongdae offers a unique space to create your own leather goods and learn leather crafting. Visitors can systematically learn leather craft through various classes, including material and reinforcement lessons, and even participate in a special tour of Sinseol-dong Leather Market for self-learners. Under the gentle and witty guidance of the workshop owner, you can craft your own one-of-a-kind leather items. This experience is highly recommended for friends, couples, or individuals looking to discover a new hobby and create something special with their own hands.
