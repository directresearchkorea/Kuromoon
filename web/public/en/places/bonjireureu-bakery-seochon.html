Bonjireureu Bakery Seochon

A large, beautifully designed bakery cafe in Seochon, specializing in bread.

Bonjireureu Bakery Seochon, a newly opened branch in Seochon, is a large cafe specializing in bakery items, carrying on the reputation of its Seongsu counterpart with a wide array of breads. Visitors can browse and taste various breads displayed from the entrance, enjoying a spacious and comfortable environment. Signature salt bread varieties are consistently popular, and seasonal specials like pistachio bread are also worth trying. With its beautiful interior and ample space, it's ideal for solo study sessions or for friends and couples to enjoy delicious bread and coffee together. Please note that the cafe is closed on the second and fourth Tuesdays of each month, so it's advisable to check before visiting.
