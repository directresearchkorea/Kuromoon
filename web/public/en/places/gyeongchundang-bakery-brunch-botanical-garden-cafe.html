Large botanical garden cafe in Icheon offering bakery and brunch.

Gyeongchundang Bakery & Brunch Botanical Garden Cafe is a large, plant-themed bakery and brunch cafe located in Bubal-eup, Icheon. Visitors can experience healing in a spacious area filled with lush greenery, enjoying various seating options including floor seating next to a grand staircase. The rice bakery items are a signature, with Manju being particularly popular as a gift, making them a must-try or purchase. Its grand yet cozy atmosphere is perfect for dates with friends or couples, family outings, or even for those seeking a peaceful solo retreat. With a second floor also available, future visits promise an even more diverse spatial experience.
