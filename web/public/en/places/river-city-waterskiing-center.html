River City Waterskiing Center

🔥 Over 353 Naver blog reviews
✅ Recent status: 운영 중

A unique water leisure facility on the Han River where you can enjoy various water sports.

River City Waterskiing Center offers a unique urban water leisure experience on Seoul's Han River. Here, visitors can enjoy a variety of water sports including waterskiing, wakeboarding, wakesurfing, paddleboarding, kayaking, and wakefoiling, with lessons readily available for beginners. While no specific signature items or photo zones are mentioned, the thrill of engaging in water sports against the scenic Han River backdrop naturally creates the best memories and photo opportunities. This spot is perfect for an active and refreshing time, feeling the cool breeze on the open river, making it highly recommended for friends, couples, families, and anyone looking for a new challenge. Operating daily from 6 AM to 8 PM, it's advisable to inquire in advance for lessons to ensure a smooth experience.
