Object Seogyo Branch in Hongdae, a goods shop offering small joys in daily life.

Object Seogyo Branch in Hongdae is a goods shop and pop-up store that brings small joys to everyday life. It's a space where visitors can discover a variety of design items and unique goods, offering a special shopping experience. Conveniently located within walking distance from Hongdae Entrance Station, it is highly recommended for those seeking distinctive and stylish items to invigorate their daily routine. From stationery to lifestyle products, a diverse range of goods allows visitors to explore their personal tastes and enjoy finding special gifts for loved ones.
