Indoor animal theme park in Times Square, Yeongdeungpo.

Zoorung Zoorung Animal Park Yeongdeungpo Branch is an indoor animal theme park where visitors can interact with various animals and enjoy ecological experience learning in the city. Guests can have a special experience by meeting various animals up close and engaging in direct interaction activities such as feeding. The signature of this place is the encounter with living animals themselves, offering rich educational content that stimulates children's curiosity. Designed as a comfortable and safe indoor space, it is highly recommended for families who want to have a fun time with their children regardless of the weather. The regular admission fee is ₩29,000, but there are age-specific discounts, so it is advisable to prepare supporting documents in advance.
