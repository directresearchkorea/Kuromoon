A pottery workshop in Seongsu-dong for unique date experiences making ceramic cups.

Craft Practice is a pottery studio located in Seongsu-dong, Seoul, offering a unique and special experience in daily life. Visitors can enjoy the creative process of touching and shaping clay to create their own one-of-a-kind ceramic cups. The self-made ceramic cup serves as a signature keepsake, offering both cherished memories and practical use. With its cozy and comfortable atmosphere, it's perfect for a unique date course for couples or creating special memories with friends. Operating hours differ between weekdays and weekends, so it's advisable to check the schedule in advance, especially for weekend visits.
