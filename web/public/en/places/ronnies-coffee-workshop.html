A coffee workshop in Uijeongbu's Yonghyeon-dong offering latte art one-day classes.

Ronnie's Coffee Workshop in Yonghyeon-dong, Uijeongbu, offers a unique opportunity to experience the artistic side of coffee through its latte art one-day classes.
