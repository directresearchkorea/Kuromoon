Chef relay dining pop-up at Lotte Department Store Jamsil.

The tvN Street Restaurant Fighter Chef Relay Pop-up is a unique dining event where chefs compete in a relay format. Visitors can enjoy a gourmet experience by tasting special menus presented by renowned chefs like Kim Ho-yoon and Kwak Dong-hoon, with Chef Kwak Dong-hoon's Chimichang being a must-try signature dish. Held at the B1F Open Dining Pop-up Event Hall of Lotte Department Store Jamsil, this experience is recommended for friends or couples seeking a new culinary adventure. The event runs from July 17 to September 17, 2026, and is available from 10:30 AM on Mondays through Thursdays.
