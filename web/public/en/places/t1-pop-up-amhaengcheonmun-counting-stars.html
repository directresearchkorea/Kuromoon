T1 Pop-up - Amhaengcheonmun: Counting Stars

🔥 Over 107 Naver blog reviews
✅ Recent status: 운영 중

A unique pop-up in Seongsu, expressing T1 players' championship records as stars.

The T1 Amhaengcheonmun: Counting Stars pop-up offers a unique narrative by symbolizing T1 players' championship records as 'stars' in an distinctive space. Visitors can immerse themselves in a unique world where five T1 players are 'star hunters,' enjoying an intriguing experience of managing the numerous stars (championship records) they've achieved. The pop-up features various exhibits and photo zones symbolizing T1 players' victories, and visitors can enjoy complimentary coffee at the T Factory lounge. This space provides a refreshing break from the bustling Seongsu area, especially engaging for League of Legends fans, making it ideal for creating special memories with friends or partners. Walk-ins are available on weekends, and don't miss the complimentary coffee offered at the T Factory Seongsu lounge.
