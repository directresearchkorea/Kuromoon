Specialty drip coffee roastery cafe in Huam-dong, Yongsan.

El Cafe Coffee Roasters, located in Huam-dong, Yongsan, is a specialty drip coffee roastery cafe. It offers a profound coffee experience that draws visitors even on rainy days for a roastery cafe tour. Freshly roasted and brewed coffee is highlighted as the signature menu item here. This spot is ideal for coffee enthusiasts to enjoy high-quality specialty coffee and relax, perfect for visiting with friends or a partner. Parking directly in front of the cafe is limited to two cars, so utilizing nearby public parking is recommended for convenience.
