Large gallery cafe in Gimpo with green views, exhibitions, and a maze garden.

Forerium, nestled in Yangchon-eup, Gimpo, is a spacious gallery cafe where lush nature and artistic elements beautifully converge. Visitors can immerse themselves in a rich visual experience, exploring not only the cafe and bakery but also various exhibition areas, a charming maze garden, and a serene greenhouse. Renowned as an "egg tart hotspot," it's an ideal place to unwind with delightful desserts and capture memorable photos against the verdant backdrop. The expansive and inviting atmosphere offers a refreshing escape for friends, couples, and families alike, making it perfect for anyone seeking a blend of art and relaxation in a natural setting. The cafe operates from 10:00 AM to 9:00 PM, with the last order at 8:30 PM, which is helpful to note for your visit.
