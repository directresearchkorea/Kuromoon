Jeonjuneun Jeonju Hanok Village Main Branch

Spacious and pleasant vegan dining spot in Jeonju Hanok Village.

Located in Jeonju Hanok Village, 'Jeonjuneun Jeonju' Main Branch is a special place where you can enjoy vegan dining in a spacious and pleasant environment. Divided into two floors, the restaurant offers a splendid yet cozy atmosphere with traditional Korean lantern (cheongsachorong) decorations that harmonize with the charm of the Hanok Village. It is highly recommended for travelers visiting Jeonju or Hanok Village, offering a unique culinary experience with healthy and delicious vegan dishes. Enjoy a leisurely meal in this place where traditional beauty and modern comfort coexist.
