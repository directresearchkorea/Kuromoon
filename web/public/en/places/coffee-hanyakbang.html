Retro-themed cafe located in an alley in Euljiro, offering unique ambiance.

Coffee Hanyakbang, nestled in an Euljiro alley, offers a unique experience by blending a distinctive 'hanyakbang' (traditional pharmacy) concept with retro sensibilities. The antique interior and props create a visual delight, making visitors feel as if they've traveled back in time, allowing them to enjoy a leisurely time in a cozy atmosphere. A variety of desserts and baked goods are available, perfect for a sweet treat with coffee, and the retro space itself serves as an excellent photo zone. This charming space, rich with old-world charm, is ideal for unique dates with friends or partners, or for enjoying solitary reflection. Parking is not available, so visitors should use public transportation or nearby paid parking lots.
