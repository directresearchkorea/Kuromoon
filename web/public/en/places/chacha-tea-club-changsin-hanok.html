A tranquil Hanok cafe in Dongdaemun offering a tea ceremony experience.

Chacha Tea Club Changsin Hanok is a tranquil Hanok cafe near Dongdaemun Station, boasting a unique concept where visitors can enjoy a traditional tea ceremony experience in a serene atmosphere. Guests can spend leisurely time appreciating sensory props like acrylic mobiles within the beautiful Hanok space, offering an ambiance so charming it's compared to the Bukchon Lounge. The acrylic mobile, a signature prop here, enhances the space's charm and serves as a captivating photo zone for visitors. With its ideal Hanok setting for dates, it is highly recommended for couples looking to create special memories in a peaceful environment. Be aware that severe waiting lines can occur, especially on weekends, so planning your visit in advance is advisable.
