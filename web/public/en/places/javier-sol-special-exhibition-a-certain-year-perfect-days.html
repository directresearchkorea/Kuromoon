The "Javier Solá Special Exhibition: A Certain Year - Perfect Days" is a unique exhibition held at Exhibition Hall 1 of the Seoul Calligraphy Art Museum at the Seoul Arts Center, illuminating the profound artistic world of Javier Solá. Visitors can experience the artist's journey and inspiration by appreciating works from a specific period, as suggested by the subtitle 'A Certain Year'. This exhibition intensively showcases the artist's distinctive vision and expression through his representative works, promising to leave a deep impression on art enthusiasts. It is highly recommended for friends, couples, or individuals seeking a deep artistic experience in a calm and contemplative atmosphere. The exhibition runs from July 10, 2026, to October 17, 2026, with a closing time of 6 PM, which visitors should note when planning their visit.
