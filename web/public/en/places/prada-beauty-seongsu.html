Prada Beauty Seongsu offers a luxury cosmetic experience.

Prada Beauty Seongsu is a special space where visitors can directly experience Prada's luxury beauty line. Guests can try various high-end cosmetics, including the Prada Reveal Mesh Cushion, Reflection Gloss, and Touch Blush, and enjoy the unique experience of receiving free photo cards and MagSafe holders. It is highly recommended to try the new gloss-textured lip products and apply the signature items directly. With a sophisticated and luxurious ambiance, it's perfect for visiting with friends or partners to explore the latest beauty trends, or for solo exploration of new cosmetics. As it may operate as a pop-up, checking relevant information before visiting is advisable.
