A tranquil hanok-themed cafe in Won-gok-myeon, Anseong.

Simsang in Won-gok-myeon, Anseong, is a tranquil hanok-themed cafe, offering a space where visitors can fully enjoy the serene beauty of traditional Korean architecture. Its spacious yard and beautiful Chinese trumpet vine (Neungsohwa) photo spot provide visual delight, making it an ideal place for families to relax and heal. During the blooming season of the Chinese trumpet vine, capturing memorable photos at this signature photo zone is highly recommended. The serene and peaceful interior atmosphere makes it perfect for dates, gatherings with friends, or even quiet solo time. The cafe is closed every Monday, and convenient parking is available for visitors.
