Garden cafe in Yongin blending plants and art

G.N Art Space is a unique cultural complex themed around plants, art, and leisurely strolls. Visitors can explore and purchase various plants and pottery within its beautifully designed, award-winning architectural space, while also enjoying beverages at the Hyde Park Garden Cafe. It is a must-visit destination for those who appreciate plants and pots, offering a special experience in a nature-friendly atmosphere. This venue is perfect for individuals seeking quiet contemplation or for couples and friends planning a relaxed weekend outing. For an enriched cultural and artistic experience, consider visiting the nearby Baeknamjun Art Center located just across the street.
