Lala Gayageum Studio in Yeongdeungpo offers a one-day gayageum class to learn Arirang.

Lala Gayageum Studio offers a unique experience to easily and enjoyably learn the beautiful traditional Korean instrument, Gayageum. Even beginners can complete a full song like 'Arirang' through a one-day class, deeply immersing themselves in the charm of the Gayageum. Completing 'Arirang' is a signature experience that provides visitors with a sense of accomplishment and creates special memories. This studio is perfect for adults seeking a new hobby or a unique experience amidst their daily routine, offering a calm and focused atmosphere for learning. As it operates on a 100% reservation basis, it is recommended to book in advance via Naver Reservation, and those coming by car should use nearby parking facilities.
