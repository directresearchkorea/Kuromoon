Delicious taco bowl specialty store at The Hyundai Seoul

Beetle Taco is a Mexican-concept dining spot located on B1F of The Hyundai Seoul, specializing in taco bowls made with fresh ingredients. Visitors can easily enjoy unique taco bowls, making it a welcome option for those who missed the taco bowls previously offered at Subway. The signature menu item here is the taco bowl, available with various toppings, allowing for a diverse taste experience tailored to individual preferences. With a casual and lively department store food court atmosphere, it's perfect for a light meal with friends or a partner, and also suitable for solo diners. It's advisable to check the operating days before visiting, for instance via Naver Maps, as they may vary depending on The Hyundai Seoul's department store holidays.
