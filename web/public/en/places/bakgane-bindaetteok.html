A famous mung bean pancake restaurant in Gwangjang Market.

This renowned Bindaetteok specialty restaurant is located in Gwangjang Market, a beloved culinary destination for many. Its signature mung bean pancakes are exceptionally crispy on the outside and moist on the inside, made with fresh ingredients that appeal to all ages. It is highly recommended for families, offering a comfortable dining experience for children and a vibrant market atmosphere. Beyond the famous Bindaetteok, visitors can also enjoy various market delicacies like fresh yukhoe (beef tartare), providing a rich gastronomic experience.
